Council posture

Keep the foundation. Wait on rollout.

The engineering base and private-link hardening are now stronger than the durable proof record. The next design review should keep the safety gate visible while the protected owner/learner pass proves the workflow with masked screenshots.

Keep

Private-link core is worth preserving

Random access codes, generated and hashed PINs, expiry, revoke, signed-in claims, claim hijack blocking, no-store APIs, noindex/no-referrer route metadata, attempt throttling, session-only PIN memory, masked reports, and public safe-share blockers are already proven in the packet.

Wait

Promotion needs protected pass proof

The current gate needs one owner fresh-link/PIN pass, learner open, learner PIN submit/account claim, assignment snapshot, durable masked report review, and at least four protected screenshots before this becomes a broader classroom workflow.

Do not publish

Private links are not public sharing

The owner flow can copy link and PIN together. That should stay a one-time owner-only moment, with durable reports, analytics, screenshots, and design artifacts masked.

Caveats

Boundaries for this preview

This is a local decision surface, not a production route. It should help us decide what to redesign next.

All private codes and PINs shown in screenshots are test fixtures. No real student private link, account secret, or production PIN was used.

Protected evidence gap

The current packet has zero real owner/learner evidence rows and zero protected screenshots. Those are the live gate blockers, not the earlier route-metadata work.

Profile caveat

The profile-after-claim capture includes compact controls outside the private-link gate. They are logged as context, not as blockers for this lane.
