Entering the Mall

Risa enters a large shopping mall in the US. It is busy, bright, and full of shops. She wants to find a map or get directions.

Continue as You.

What do you usually do when you get lost in a new place? Do you ask someone for help, or do you prefer to look at your smartphone map?

Useful phrases

I’m a little lost.

Where is the ~?

information desk / restroom / elevator

Right over there.

pointing to a place nearby

Excuse me

Always use this to be polite!

Starter help

I usually ask someone at the information desk first.

I check the map near the entrance if I can find one.

I ask for help because I do not want to get more lost.

One model reply

I usually ask someone near the information desk because it is faster than walking around by myself.

More acceptable replies

I look for a mall map first, and then I ask a staff member if I still feel lost.

I use my phone map, but I still ask a person if the mall is very big.

Tip: Try to reuse: I’m a little lost., Where is the ~? (information desk / restroom / elevator), Right over there. (pointing to a place nearby), Excuse me (Always use this to be polite!).

Continue The Conversation

Say the whole visit simply

Continue as You.

Describe the mall visit in order from entering to leaving. Use short clear sentences.

Starter help

First, I would...

I'd ask where the ... is first.

After that, I would...

At the end, I would...

One model reply

First, I would ask for help. Then I would buy jeans, eat lunch, get groceries, and have coffee before I go home.

More acceptable replies

First I would find the food court, and later I would buy groceries.

I would try on the jeans, then order lunch, and after that I would pay for the groceries.

Tip: Keep the order clear: directions, clothes, food, groceries, then coffee or going home.

Clothing Store (Trying Clothes)

Risa finds a clothing store. She wants to buy a new pair of jeans but needs to check the fit and price.

Continue as You.

Do you like trying on clothes before you buy them, or do you shop online? What is your favorite piece of clothing to buy?

Useful phrases

I’m looking for ~

Do you have this in [color]?

Where is the fitting room?

This is too tight / too loose / too long / too short.

One size up / one size down.

I'll take it / I'll take them.

Use this when you decide to buy

Starter help

I like trying clothes on before I buy them.

I usually ask for one size up if something feels tight.

I check the fit first and then I decide about the price.

One model reply

I like trying clothes on first because I want to check the fit, and I ask for one size up if they feel tight.

More acceptable replies

I usually try two sizes because one can feel too tight and the other can feel better.

I shop online too, but for jeans I prefer the fitting room so I can check the size.

Tip: Try to reuse: I’m looking for ~, Do you have this in [color]?, Where is the fitting room?, This is too tight / too loose / too long / too short..

Food Court (Ordering Food)

Shopping makes Risa hungry. She goes to the busy food court to get a quick lunch.

Continue as You.

What is your favorite food to eat at a food court? Do you usually eat "for here" or take your food "to go"?

Useful phrases

I’ll have ~

Make it a combo

Adding a side and a drink

For here or to go?

Eating at the restaurant vs. taking it home

Cash or card?

That’ll be ~

Telling you the total price

Starter help

I usually eat for here if I want a short break.

I order a combo when I am very hungry.

I choose simple food that is fast to eat in the mall.

One model reply

I usually eat for here, and I often choose a combo meal because it is easy and practical in a busy mall.

More acceptable replies

My favorite food-court lunch is a sandwich and fries, and I usually pay by card.

I take it to go when I am in a hurry, but I eat there if I want to rest.

Tip: Try to reuse: I’ll have ~, Make it a combo (Adding a side and a drink), For here or to go? (Eating at the restaurant vs. taking it home), Cash or card?.

Continue The Conversation

Change one thing politely

Continue as You.

Ask one short polite question when something is different, like the wrong size, a missing item, or a long line.

Starter help

Could I try one size up?

Could I get a smaller one?

Do you have any cheaper options?

Could I also have the receipt?

More starter phrases
  • If the line is long, I would...

One model reply

Could I try one size up? If not, I can look at another pair.

More acceptable replies

Do you have any bread today? If not, I can buy rice instead.

Could I pay by card here? If not, I will use cash.

Tip: One short polite repair makes the conversation sound more real.

Continue as You.

Standard cashier greeting

I buy simple food like milk and fruit, and I ask for a bag only if I really need one.

I use Apple Pay when I can because it is fast at the checkout.

Coffee Break + Small Talk

Risa is tired from walking. She sits at a café near the exit to rest before going home.

Continue as You.

Are you comfortable making "small talk" with strangers, like in an elevator or a coffee line? What is a safe and polite topic to talk about with a stranger in your country?

Useful phrases

Hot or iced?

Can I get a name? / What name?

It’s really crowded / busy.

Did you find anything good?

It’s my first time.

Have a great day! / You too!

Starter help

A safe topic is how busy the mall is today.

I can talk about the weather or the long line.

I usually keep small talk short and friendly.

One model reply

A safe topic is how busy the mall is today, because it is easy to answer and still sounds friendly.

More acceptable replies

I can say the line is long or the weather is nice, and then ask one simple question back.

I prefer short small talk, so I talk about the sale or ask if the other person found anything good.

Tip: Try to reuse: Hot or iced?, Can I get a name? / What name?, It’s really crowded / busy., Did you find anything good?.
