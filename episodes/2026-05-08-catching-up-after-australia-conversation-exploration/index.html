Continue The Conversation

Build your full trip answer

Continue as You.

Answer "How was Australia?" for 45 to 60 seconds. Include who, where, one best moment, one feeling, and one comparison.

Useful phrases

I have just come back from Australia, and...

I went with...

One thing I remember clearly is...

Compared with Japan, ...

Starter help

I have just come back from Australia, and...

I went with...

One thing I remember clearly is...

Compared with Japan, ...

One model reply

I have just come back from Australia. I went with my family, and it was relaxing but also busy. One thing I remember clearly is the harbour because it felt very open. Compared with Japan, some places felt slower and more relaxed.

Focus: past simple questions | clarification repair

Tip: Do not try to tell everything. Choose three useful details and connect them with because, but, and compared with.
