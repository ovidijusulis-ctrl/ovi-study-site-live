Conversation Questions

Café and Business Ideas

Main question: Did you talk about franchising your café?

Follow-up questions

  • What ideas did you discuss?
  • Did the idea feel realistic?
  • What would be the first step?
  • Do you think your café concept could work in Australia?
  • Which part of the concept would be attractive?
  • Would the charcoal latte work there?
  • What would you need to change for Australian customers?
  • Would menu size, portion size, iced drinks, brunch options, or interior style need to change?
  • What should stay the same?
  • Should you keep the design story, the charcoal latte, the Japanese atmosphere, or the roof-tile connection?

Follow-up questions

  • Would you start with a full café or a small market stall?
  • Which is safer for testing?

Next Steps

Could / would for business ideas

B1 · core

Use could, would, and might to discuss realistic business options without sounding too certain.

could | would | might

"I think it could work because..."

Could makes the business idea sound possible, not guaranteed.
