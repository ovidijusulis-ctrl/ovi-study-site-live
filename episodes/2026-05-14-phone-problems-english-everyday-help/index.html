🎯Lesson Goal

In this lesson, you will learn how to use simple English when your phone has a problem. You will practice how to ask for a charger, ask for Wi-Fi, explain a phone problem, ask someone to call you, and get help if your phone is lost or frozen.

🌍Why Phone Problem English Is Useful

Phones are useful, but phone problems can feel stressful. Your battery may be low. The Wi-Fi may not work. Your screen may freeze. In these moments, you do not need perfect English. You need calm, clear sentences. You often need to ask:

  • Can I charge my phone here?
  • What is the Wi-Fi password?
  • My phone is not working.
  • Could you call this number for me?
  • Is there a lost and found?
