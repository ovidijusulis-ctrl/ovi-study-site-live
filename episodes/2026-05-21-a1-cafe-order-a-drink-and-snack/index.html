Introduction

Cafe English is very useful because the same short sentences work in many places. You can say what you want, choose a size, and pay. If you are not ready, you can say, "One moment, please." In this lesson, Leo orders a drink and a snack. The cafe is busy, so he needs to be clear. He does not use long sentences. He uses polite words and simple choices. Good cafe English is not perfect grammar. It is clear information: item, size, here or to go, and payment.

CLOSING

A cafe order has a simple order: greeting, item, size, here or payment. You can keep your short and polite.

Useful corrections are also short. Say, "Sorry, hot, please," or "No please. " Clear is better than long.
