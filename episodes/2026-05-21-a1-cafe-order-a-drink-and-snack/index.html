Introduction

Cafe English is very useful because the same short sentences work in many places. You can say what you want, choose a size, and pay.

If you are not ready, you can say, "One moment, please."

In this lesson, Leo orders a drink and a snack. The cafe is busy, so he needs to be clear.

He does not use long sentences. He uses polite words and simple choices.

Good cafe English is not perfect grammar. It is clear information: item, size, here or to go, and payment.

Grammar help

Grammar help for this lesson

a / an / the

Basic Articles

Use a or an for one new thing. Use the when both people know which thing.

You introduce an object, then talk about that same object again.

Common mistake

Do not use a with plural nouns. Say two bags, not two a bags.

  • I need a pen. The pen on the desk is mine.
日本語で見る

初めて話す1つの物には a / an を使います。どの物か分かっている時は the を使います。

Practice this grammar

and / but / or

Basic Connectors

Use and to add, but to show a contrast, and or to give a choice.

You join two short ideas or choices.

Common mistake

Do not use but when there is no contrast.

  • I like tea and coffee, but I do not like cola.
日本語で見る

and は追加、but は反対・対比、or は選択を表します。
