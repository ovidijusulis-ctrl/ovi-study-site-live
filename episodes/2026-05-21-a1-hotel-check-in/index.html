Grammar help

Grammar help for this lesson

my / your / his / her

Possessive Adjectives

Use my, your, his, her, our, and their before a noun to show connection or belonging.

You talk about bags, names, family, tickets, rooms, or anything connected to a person.

Common mistake

Do not use he or she before a noun. Say his bag or her bag.

  • This is my bag. Is that your ticket?
日本語で見る

my, your, his, her, our, their を名詞の前に置いて、誰の物か・誰と関係があるかを表します。

Practice this grammar

in / on / under / next to

Prepositions Of Place

Use words like in, on, under, and next to to say where something is.

You give directions, find objects, or describe a room or place.

Common mistake

Do not use in for every place. Say on the chair, not in the chair, when it is on the surface.

  • The ticket is in the bag. The phone is on the table.
日本語で見る

in, on, under, next to などを使って、物がどこにあるかを言います。
