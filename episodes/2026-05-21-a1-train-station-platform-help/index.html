Grammar help

Grammar help for this lesson

instructions

Basic Imperatives

Give a short instruction. Start with the action word: Open, Wait, Listen, or Do not run.

You give classroom directions, safety instructions, or simple steps.

Common mistake

Do not add you before a normal imperative. Say Please wait, not You please wait.

  • Open the door. Please wait here.
日本語で見る

短い指示を出す形です。Open, Wait, Listen, Do not run のように動詞から始めます。

Practice this grammar

at / on / in

Prepositions Of Time

Use at for clock times, on for days/dates, and in for months, years, or seasons.

You say when a class, trip, meeting, or event happens.

Common mistake

Do not say in Monday. Say on Monday.

  • The class starts at nine on Monday in April.
日本語で見る

時刻には at、曜日や日付には on、月・年・季節には in を使います。
