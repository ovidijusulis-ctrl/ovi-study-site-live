Introduction

Home repair English is useful when you rent an apartment or stay in shared housing. You need to explain what is broken, how urgent it is, and when someone can come. In this lesson, Omar calls the building office because his kitchen sink is leaking. He describes the problem and agrees on a repair time. The language is practical: location, problem, urgency, access, and appointment time.

Final Speaking Task

Situation:Home repair English is useful when you rent an apartment or stay in shared housing. You need to explain what is broken, how urgent it is, and when someone can come.

AOmarBOffice worker
Omar

Hello. This is Omar in apartment 308. I have a problem with my kitchen sink.

Office worker

What is happening?

Omar

Water is leaking under the sink. It started last night, and now the floor is wet.

Office worker

Is the water still leaking now?

Omar

Yes, a little. I put a bowl under it, but I am worried it will get worse.

Office worker

Can you turn off the small valve under the sink?

Role-play a repair call for a leak, a broken heater, or a door lock problem.
