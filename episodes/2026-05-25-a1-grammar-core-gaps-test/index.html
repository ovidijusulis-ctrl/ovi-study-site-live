    Basic Articles

    A1 · foundation

    Use a or an for one new thing. Use the when both people know which thing.

    日本語で見る

    初めて話す1つの物には a / an を使います。どの物か分かっている時は the を使います。

    Grammar words
    article

    A small word before a noun: a, an, or the.

    名詞の前に置く小さい言葉です。a, an, the のことです。

    noun

    A word for a person, place, or thing.

    人、場所、物を表す言葉です。

    singular

    One person or one thing.

    人や物が1つだけの形です。

    plural

    More than one person or thing.

    人や物が2つ以上ある形です。

    a | an | the

    Meaning

    Use a or an for one new thing. Use the when both people know which thing.

    日本語で見る

    初めて話す1つの物には a / an を使います。どの物か分かっている時は the を使います。

    Shape

    a/an + singular noun; the + known noun

    Example

    I need a pen. The pen on the desk is mine.

    Common mistake

    Do not use a with plural nouns. Say two bags, not two a bags.

    Try it

    Say one thing you need: I need a/an _____. Then say where the thing is: The _____ is here.

    This is a new notebook.

    Why: Use a before one new singular noun.

    imperative

    Wait here や Do not run のような短い指示です。

    verb

    An action word, like go, eat, have, or wait.

    go, eat, have, wait のような動作を表す言葉です。

    open | listen | wait | please | do not

    Meaning

    Give a short instruction. Start with the action word: Open, Wait, Listen, or Do not run.

    verb

    go, eat, have, wait のような動作を表す言葉です。

    ability

    Something you can do: I can swim.

    Can I sit here? のように、してもいいか聞くことです。
