Final Reflection

Gelato history is not only about flavor. It is a story of trade, technology, and social space. From court foods and elite treats to shared cafés and machine-scale production, the same dessert changed many times. That is the pattern: tradition can stay strong when it can adapt to new places. Final Comprehension Question: What are the main stages in the history of gelato? Final Open Question: Why can a simple food like gelato become part of cultural identity while still changing in new places?
