Lesson Goal

Ask about a city office form in simple English. Explain what you need, ask which part to fill in, check the documents, and confirm where to submit it.

Introduction

Forms can make students nervous. The page may have many boxes, and the office worker may speak quickly.

The best strategy is to ask about one part at a time.

This lesson follows Sara. She moved to a new apartment.

She visits a city office because she needs to update her address. She has her ID card, lease, and phone.

Warm-Up Questions

  • Have you ever filled in a form in another language?
  • What part of a form feels difficult?
  • What documents do people often need at an office?
  • How can you ask for help politely?
