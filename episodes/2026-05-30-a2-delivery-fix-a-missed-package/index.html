Introduction

Use simple English when a package does not arrive.

Explain the problem, check the address, ask about the next delivery, and confirm the safe next step.

A delivery problem can feel stressful because you cannot see the package.

You may have a tracking number, a message on your phone, or a paper note.

The English does not need to be long.

It needs to be clear.

This lesson follows Lina.

She ordered a small kitchen tool.

The website says the driver came at 2:15, but she was at home.

She needs to explain the problem and choose a safe delivery option.

Warm-up: Have you ever missed a delivery?

What information do you need before you call a delivery office?

Is it easier to call, chat online, or visit a counter?

Summary

Delivery English is problem-solving English.

Say the problem first.

Check the address and time.

Ask for the next option.
