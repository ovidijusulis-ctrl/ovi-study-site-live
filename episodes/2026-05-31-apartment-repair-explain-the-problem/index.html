Introduction

Learn how to contact an apartment repair office and explain a problem at home.

Say where the problem is.

Give simple details about when it started.

Explain how serious it is.

Arrange a repair time.

Confirm the next step.

Maria lives in an apartment.

One morning, she sees water under the kitchen sink.

The water is slowly leaking onto the floor.

She decides to contact the apartment repair office.

A staff member answers the phone.

Maria needs to explain the problem clearly so the repair office can help her.

As the conversation continues, she explains where the problem is, when it started, how serious it is, and when a repair worker can visit.
