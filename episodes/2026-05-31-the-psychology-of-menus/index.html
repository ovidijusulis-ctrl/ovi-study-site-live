Technical glossary 6 terms

attention path

the order in which a customer notices things

Strong positions and boxes can guide the first look.

uncertainty reduction

making a choice feel less risky

Photos and clear labels help unfamiliar food feel safer.

emotional cue

a design signal that creates a feeling

Colors and words can make food feel casual, fresh, or luxurious.

social proof

a signal that many people like something

"Most Popular" can reduce decision stress.

perceived value

the feeling that a price is worth it

Set meals can feel like a better deal than separate items.

price anchor

a high price that changes comparison

A costly item can make a middle item seem reasonable.
