    Articles and quantifiers

    A1 · Shopping sentences

    Choose a/an for one countable thing, some for an amount, and many for a large number.

    日本語で見る

    a/an は1つの数えられる物、some は量やいくつか、many は数えられる物がたくさんある時に使います。

    Grammar words
    a/an

    one thing

    1つ

    some

    an amount or more than one

    いくつか・ある量

    many

    a large number

    たくさん

    a | an | some | many

    "Use a or an for one thing: a basket, a shopping cart, an apple."

    Use a or an when you talk about one countable thing.

    Jump to transcript

    "Use some for more than one thing or an amount: some apples, some milk, some snacks."

    Use some for amounts and plural shopping items.

    Jump to transcript

    "Use many for a large number of countable things: many families, many supermarkets, many bags."

    Use many before countable plural nouns.

    Choose

    I buy ___ apple.

    Use an before one apple.

    Show answer

    an

    Tip: One apple starts with a vowel sound.

    Use an before one apple.

    • a/an is for one thing; some is for an amount; many is for a large number.

    Choose

    She buys ___ milk.

    Use some before milk when you mean an amount.

    Show answer

    some

    Tip: Milk is an amount here.

    Use some before milk when you mean an amount.

    • Use some with milk, rice, water, and other amounts.
    Jump to transcript

    Choose

    ___ families go shopping every week.

    Use many before countable plural nouns like families.

    Show answer

    Many

    Tip: Families are countable and plural.

    Use many before countable plural nouns like families.

    • Use many with countable plural nouns: families, supermarkets, bags.

    Final Reflection

    You do not need long English at a supermarket. One short sentence can help: "I need a basket," "I buy some milk," or "I pay by card."

    Final Speaking Task

    Useful Speaking Patterns

    • I buy some...
    • I need a basket.
    • Do you have...?
    • I pay by card.

    Practice Questions

    • You are in a supermarket. What do you buy?
    • Do you need a basket or a shopping cart?
    • How do you pay?
