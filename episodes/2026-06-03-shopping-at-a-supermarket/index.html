Supermarket English is useful for daily life. You can talk about food, shopping places, bags, and payment with short sentences.

Grammar Focus: Some, Many, and A

Use a or an for one thing: a a an

Use some for more than one thing or an some some some

Use many for a large number of things: many families, many many

    Final Reflection

    Supermarket English is everyday English. You can talk about what you buy, when you shop, what you use, and how you pay.

    Short sentences are enough: "I buy some fruit," "I use a basket," "I pay by card," and "I bring my own bag."

    Final Speaking Task

    Useful Speaking Patterns

    • I buy some fruit.
    • I need a basket.
    • I use a shopping cart.
    • I pay with cash.
    • I pay by card.
    • I pay with my phone.
    • I bring my own bag.
    • My favorite thing to buy is...

    Practice Questions

    • What can people buy at supermarkets?
    • Are supermarkets bigger or smaller than convenience stores?
    • What do people use when shopping?
    • How do people pay at the checkout?
    • What do you like to buy at a supermarket?
