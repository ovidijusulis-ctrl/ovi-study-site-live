Lesson Goal

Use simple English to ask again and confirm transport information when the answer is fast.

Introduction

This lesson continues the earlier transport lesson, but it does not practice every transport problem.

Today the traveler has one connected job: hear the answer, ask again, and confirm the next step.

The traveler is at Central Station. The plan is simple: take the Green Line, change at City Hall, and get off at Riverside.

The English is also simple. The useful phrases are, "Sorry, one more time?",

"So, Platform 2?", and "Do I change at City Hall?"

Warm-Up Questions

  • Do station staff sometimes speak too fast?
  • What word can you say when you need the answer again?
  • What information is important: line, platform, time, or stop?
  • Do you usually repeat an answer to check it?
