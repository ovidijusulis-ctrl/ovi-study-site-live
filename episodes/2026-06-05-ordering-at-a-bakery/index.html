Final Reflection

Bakery English can be very short. Choose the food, say for here or takeaway, and pay.

Useful sentences are: "This one, please," "Takeaway, please," and "I will pay by card."

Useful Speaking Patterns

  • This one, please.
  • I will take...
  • For here, please.
  • Takeaway, please.
  • I will pay by...
