Final Reflection

Post-office English is about the item, the address, the price, and the arrival time.

Useful sentences are: "I want to send this package," "How much is postage?", and "Can I have a tracking number?"

Useful Speaking Patterns

  • Is this address okay?
  • How much is postage?
  • Can I have a tracking number?
  • When will it arrive?
