💡Grammar check5 checks

use "to" before the destination. Say "Please take me to the hotel." and "I need to go to the station."

  • Please take me to the hotel.
  • I need to go to the station.
  • Please take me to Central Station.

Try: Please take me to Central Station.

1. Which taxi sentence is correct?

2. Choose the best word: Please take me ___ the station.

3. Complete: I need to go ___ the airport.

4. Which sentence is natural?

5. Say one new sentence with to + a destination.
