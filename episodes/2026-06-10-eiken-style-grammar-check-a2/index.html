    at, on, and in 2 checks

    Rule: at, on, and in

    Time and place clues

    Choose the word from the clue type, not from guessing.

    at exact time or exact point at 4:30 / at the station
    on day, date, or surface on Wednesday / on the wall
    in month, year, room, city, or inside place in July / in my bag

    Careful Do not use in for an exact clock time.

    Check Ask: is the clue a clock time, a day, or an inside place?

    日本語で見る

    時刻や場所の種類を見て選びます。正確な時刻は at、曜日や表面は on、月や中にある場所は in です。

    The quiz starts at 4:30.

    Why: Use at because 4:30 is an exact clock time.

    Transcript line

    We study on Wednesday and in July.

    Why: Use on for a day. Use in for a month.

    because and so 2 checks

    Rule: because and so

    Reason or result

    Choose the connector by the job of the next idea.

    because introduces the reason I stayed home because I was sick.
    so introduces the result It was raining, so we took a taxi.

    Careful Because and so do not do the same job.

    Check Ask: does the next idea answer why, or tell what happened next?

    日本語で見る

    理由を言うなら because、結果を言うなら so を使います。

    I stayed home because I had a fever.

    Why: Use because before the reason.

    Transcript line

    The train was late, so I sent a message.

    Why: Use so before the result.
