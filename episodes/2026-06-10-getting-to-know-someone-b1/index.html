🎧Listening NoteHelp

This is a speaking-first question sheet. It does not have a story, article, or normal Listen mode. A teacher can choose 6-8 cards and build a simple profile of the person from their answers. Teacher flow: Start with safe present-life questions. Move to interests, past experiences, goals, and communication style only if the student seems comfortable. After each answer, ask one detail question and one feeling/opinion question.

💡Key IdeaHelp

A good conversation is not a list of random questions.

It is a small map of the person: daily life, people, interests, experiences, goals, and comfortable boundaries.
