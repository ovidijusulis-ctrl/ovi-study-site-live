Final Reflection

Video-game English can be simple. You can name the kind of game, say what players do, and give one reason.

Short sentences are "I like adventure games," "Players new places," "I play on and "I like team games because I can play with friends."

Useful Speaking Patterns

  • I like playing...

  • My favorite kind of game is...

  • In this game, players can...

  • I play games after...

  • I like this game because...

  • I play / with friends.
