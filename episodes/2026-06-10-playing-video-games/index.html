Playing Video Games

Introduction

Introduction

Video games are a normal for many people. You learn simple English for , game types, friends, and safe . You also see a few real examples before practicing a longer conversation between two friends choosing a game.

Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you play games on a phone, computer, console, or tablet?

  2. Do you like playing alone or with friends?

  3. What kind of game is fun for you?

Reading

1. Where People Play

People can play video games on different platforms. A platform is the place or device you use to play. A phone is easy to carry. A computer can be good for many online games. A console connects to a TV or screen. A tablet can feel simple and relaxed. Some people play for a short time. They play one quick game on the train or at home. Other people play for a longer time with friends. The important English is simple: name the platform, name the game, and say when you play.

Check your understanding

1. What is a platform?

Answer

A platform is the place or device you use to play a game.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I usually play on my phone. I play on a computer. I play on a console. I do not play games often.

Think one step further

  • What platform do you usually use?

Reading

Useful Speaking Patterns: Platforms

- Which platform should we use? - Let's play on... - I can play on my tablet. - I can play on my computer.

Reading

2. Kinds of Games

There are many kinds of games. In an action game, players move quickly and make fast choices. In a sports or racing game, players can play soccer, basketball, or car races. In an adventure game, players explore new places and find useful items. Real examples help. Minecraft is a building and survival game: players use blocks to make houses, farms, or whole worlds. Mario Kart is a racing game: players drive, use items, and try to finish first. Animal Crossing is a calm life game: players decorate, catch fish, and visit neighbors. The Legend of Zelda is an adventure series: players explore, find tools, and solve puzzles.

Check your understanding

1. Name two real game examples from this section and their game types.

Answer

Minecraft is a building and survival game, and Mario Kart is a racing game. Animal Crossing is a calm life game, and The Legend of Zelda is an adventure series.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I like Minecraft because I can build. I like Mario Kart because it is exciting. I like Animal Crossing because it is calm. I like The Legend of Zelda because I can explore.

Think one step further

  • Which real game or game type do you like best?

Reading

Useful Speaking Patterns: Game Types

- What kind of game do you want to play? - I like Minecraft because I can build. - I like Mario Kart because it is exciting. - I like Animal Crossing because it is calm.

Reading

3. Playing with Friends

Some games are single-player games. One person plays alone. This can be good when you want quiet time. Some games are team games. Friends work together, talk, help each other, and make a plan. Playing with friends needs simple communication. Friends can choose the game, agree on the time, and decide if they want voice chat or short messages. Good game English is friendly, clear, and not too long.

Check your understanding

1. What do friends do in team games?

Answer

Friends work together, talk, help each other, and make a plan.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I prefer single-player games because... I prefer team games because... It depends on the game.

Think one step further

  • Do you prefer single-player games or team games?

Reading

Useful Speaking Patterns: Playing with Friends

- Do you want to play a game later? - Can we play without voice chat today? - We can use short messages. - That works for me.

Reading

4. Choosing a Good Game

Friends do not always want the same game. One friend may want a fast game like Mario Kart. Another friend may want a calm game like Animal Crossing or a building game like Minecraft. It is useful to explain the reason, not only the game name. Friends can also make a small plan before they start. They choose the platform, choose the game type, choose the time, and choose one rule for playing safely.

Check your understanding

1. What four things can friends choose before they play?

Answer

They can choose the platform, game type, time, and one safe rule.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I would choose a calm game. I would choose a team game. I would play for thirty minutes. I would take a break after one round.

Think one step further

  • What game would you choose with a friend today?

Reading

Useful Speaking Patterns: Choosing and Closing

- I want something relaxing today. - I want something exciting today. - Not Mario Kart tonight. - Maybe Minecraft in creative mode. - Let's play for thirty minutes. - I need a break after this round. - See you then. - Talk to you later.

Roleplay

Full after-school conversation Role-play

Practice once. Then change one detail: the platform, the game type, the time, or the reason.

Mika

Do you want to play a game later?

Leo

Sure. What kind of game do you want to play?

Mika

I want something relaxing today.

Leo

Okay. Not Mario Kart tonight?

Mika

Not today. I am a little tired.

Leo

Then maybe Minecraft in creative mode?

Mika

Good idea. We can make a small town.

Leo

Which platform should we use?

Mika

I can play on my tablet.

Leo

I can play on my computer. Is that okay?

Mika

Yes, that works. Can we play without voice chat today?

Leo

Sure. We can use short messages.

Mika

Great. How long can you play?

Leo

About thirty minutes.

Mika

Perfect. After one round, let's take a short break.

Leo

Good plan. What should we build first?

Mika

Let's build a park and a small cafe.

Leo

Nice. I can build the park.

Mika

And I can build the cafe.

Leo

Great. Let's start at seven.

Mika

Perfect. I will be online at seven.

Leo

Nice. See you then.

Mika

See you then. Have a good break.

Leo

Thanks. Talk to you later.

Final Speaking Task

  1. What platform do you usually use for games?

  2. Which real game or game type do you like best?

  3. Why do you like that kind of game?

  4. Do you prefer single-player games or team games?

  5. What safe habit is useful when friends play online?

  6. Roleplay two friends choosing a game. Then change one detail.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Video-game English does not need to be complicated. You can name the platform, name the kind of game, give one reason, and make a small plan with a friend.

When you talk about games in English, try this order: what game, what platform, why you like it, how long you can play, and one friendly rule.
