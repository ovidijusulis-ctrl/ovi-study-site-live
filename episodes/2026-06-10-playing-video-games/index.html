Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you play games on a phone, computer, console, or tablet?

  2. Do you like playing alone or with friends?

  3. What kind of game is fun for you?

Reading

1. Where People Play

People can play video games on different platforms. A platform is the place or device you use to play.

A phone is easy to carry. A computer can be good for many online games.

A console connects to a TV or screen. A tablet can feel simple and relaxed.

Some people play for a short time. They play one quick game on the train or at home.

Other people play for a longer time with friends. The important English is simple: name the platform, name the game, and say when you play.

2. Kinds of Games

There are many kinds of games. In an action game, players move quickly and make fast choices.

In a sports or racing game, players can play soccer, basketball, or car races. In an adventure game, players explore new places and find useful items.

Real examples help. Minecraft is a building and survival game: players use blocks to make houses, farms, or whole worlds.

Mario Kart is a racing game: players drive, use items, and try to finish first. Animal Crossing is a calm life game: players decorate, catch fish, and visit neighbors. The Legend of Zelda is an adventure series: players explore, find tools, and solve puzzles.

3. Playing with Friends

Some games are single-player games. One person plays alone.

This can be good when you want quiet time. Some games are team games.

Friends work together, talk, help each other, and make a plan. Playing with friends needs simple communication.

Friends can choose the game, agree on the time, and decide if they want voice chat or short messages. Good game English is friendly, clear, and not too long.

4. Choosing a Good Game

Friends do not always want the same game. One friend may want a fast game like Mario Kart.

Another friend may want a calm game like Animal Crossing or a building game like Minecraft. It is useful to explain the reason, not only the game name.

Friends can also make a small plan before they start. They choose the platform, choose the game type, choose the time, and choose one rule for playing safely.

Roleplay

Full after-school conversation Role-play

Practise with the dialogue audio

Audio unavailable. Please try again.

Practice once. Then change one detail: the platform, the game type, the time, or the reason.

Mika

Do you want to play a game later?

Leo

Sure. What kind of game do you want to play?

Mika

I want something relaxing today.

Leo

Okay. Not Mario Kart tonight?

Mika

Not today. I am a little tired.

Leo

Then maybe Minecraft in creative mode?

Mika

Good idea. We can make a small town.

Leo

Which platform should we use?

Mika

I can play on my tablet.

Leo

I can play on my computer. Is that okay?

Mika

Yes, that works. Can we play without voice chat today?

Leo

Sure. We can use short messages.

Mika

Great. How long can you play?

Leo

About thirty minutes.

Mika

Perfect. After one round, let's take a short break.

Leo

Good plan. What should we build first?

Mika

Let's build a park and a small cafe.

Leo

Nice. I can build the park.

Mika

And I can build the cafe.

Leo

Great. Let's start at seven.

Mika

Perfect. I will be online at seven.

Leo

Nice. See you then.

Mika

See you then. Have a good break.

Leo

Thanks. Talk to you later.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Video-game English does not need to be complicated. You can name the platform, name the kind of game, give one reason, and make a small plan with a friend.

When you talk about games in English, try this order: what game, what platform, why you like it, how long you can play, and one friendly rule.
