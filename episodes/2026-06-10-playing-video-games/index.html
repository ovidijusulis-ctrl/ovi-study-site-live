Reading

1. Where People Play

People can play video games on different platforms.

A platform is the place or device you use to play.

A phone is easy to carry.

A computer can be good for many online games.

A console connects to a TV or screen.

A tablet can feel simple and relaxed.

Some people play for a short time.

They play one quick game on the train or at home.

Other people play for a longer time with friends.

Reading

2. Kinds of Games

There are many kinds of games.

In an action game, players move quickly and make fast choices.

In a sports or racing game, players can play soccer, basketball, or car races.

In an adventure game, players explore new places and find useful items.

Real examples help.

Reading

3. Playing with Friends

Some games are single-player games.

One person plays alone.

This can be good when you want quiet time.

Some games are team games.

Friends work together, talk, help each other, and make a plan.

Playing with friends needs simple communication.

Friends can choose the game, agree on the time, and decide if they want voice chat or short messages.

Good game English is friendly, clear, and not too long.

Reading

4. Choosing a Good Game

Friends do not always want the same game.

One friend may want a fast game like Mario Kart.

Another friend may want a calm game like Animal Crossing or a building game like Minecraft.

It is useful to explain the reason, not only the game name.

Friends can also make a small plan before they start.

They choose the platform, choose the game type, choose the time, and choose one rule for playing safely.
