A2: Playing Video Games

Quality checked lesson

Learn simple English for talking about video games: where you play, what kind of game you like, why you like it, and how friends choose a game together.

Introduction

Video games are a normal for many people. You learn simple English for platforms, game types, friends, and safe habits. You also see a few real examples before practicing a longer conversation between two friends choosing a game.

Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you play games on a phone, computer, console, or tablet?

  2. Do you like playing alone or with friends?

  3. What kind of game is fun for you?

4. Choosing a Good Game

Friends do not always want the same game. One friend may want a fast game like Mario Kart. Another friend may want a calm game like Animal Crossing or a building game like Minecraft. It is useful to explain the reason, not only the game name. Friends can also make a small plan they start. They choose the platform, choose the game type, choose the time, and choose one rule for playing safely.

Check your understanding

1. What four things can friends choose before they play?

Sample answer

They can choose the platform, game type, time, and one safe rule.

Think one step further

What game would you choose with a friend today?

Helpful clue

  • I would choose a calm game.
  • I would choose a team game.
  • I would play for thirty minutes.
  • I would take a break after one round.

Leo

Okay. Not Mario Kart tonight?

Not today. I am a little tired.

Leo

Then maybe Minecraft in creative mode?

Mika

Good idea. We can make a small town.

Leo

Which platform should we use?

Mika

I can play on my tablet.

Leo

I can play on my computer. Is that okay?

Mika

Yes, that works. Can we play without voice chat today?

Leo

Sure. We can use short messages.

Mika

Great. How long can you play?

Leo

About thirty minutes.

Mika

Perfect. After one round, let's take a short break.

Leo

Good plan. What should we build first?

Mika

Leo

Nice. I can build the park.

Mika

And I can build the cafe.

Leo

Mika

Perfect. I will be online at seven.

Leo

Nice. See you then.

See you then. Have a good break.

Leo

Thanks. Talk to you later.
