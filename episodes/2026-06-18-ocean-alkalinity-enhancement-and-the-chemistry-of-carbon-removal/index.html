Summary

Ocean alkalinity enhancement is cool because it treats climate as chemistry, not only politics or engineering.

But that is also why it is difficult.

The ocean is not a lab beaker.

A responsible OAE lesson should leave learners with both curiosity and caution: the mechanism is plausible, the measurement challenge is hard, and the social permission to test it matters.
