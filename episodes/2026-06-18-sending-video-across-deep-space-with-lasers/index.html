Technical glossary 4 terms

Optical communication

Sending data with light

It can move high data volumes through narrow laser beams.

Transceiver

A device that sends and receives

The spacecraft needs one unit to handle the optical link.

Beacon

A guiding signal

It helps the spacecraft and ground station point correctly.

Hybrid network

Two systems used together

Radio and optical links can cover different mission needs.

Step

What Happens

Risk Or Limit

Encode

Mission data is prepared for optical transmission.

Data must be protected from loss and errors.

Point

The spacecraft aims a narrow laser toward Earth.

Tiny pointing errors can miss the receiver.

Receive

Ground telescopes collect the light signal.

Weather and atmosphere can interfere.

Decode

The received signal becomes usable mission data.

Weak signals require careful processing.
