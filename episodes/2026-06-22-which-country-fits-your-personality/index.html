A2: Which Country Fits Your Personality?

Different countries and travel places can feel like different moods. Some places feel quiet and organized. Some places feel lively and modern. Some places feel natural, friendly, historic, warm, or easy to use.

It helps you describe yourself, compare choices, explain reasons, and give friendly advice.
