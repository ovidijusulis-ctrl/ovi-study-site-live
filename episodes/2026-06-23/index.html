Introduction

Practice simple, natural English for likes, free time, food, colors, places, and short reasons.

Practice

What is a like?

A like is a preference or a small choice.

What is one thing you enjoy at one time but not at another time?

What is free time?

Free time is time away from work or school.

Do you like quiet free time or active free time?

What are food likes about?

Food likes are about taste and choice.

What kind of food do you like: sweet, spicy, simple, or new?

What can color show?

Color can show style, mood, or taste.

What color do you like for clothes or for a room?

What is a place preference about?

It is about feeling in a place.

What kind of place do you like for talking, walking, or resting?

What does a reason do?

A reason explains a like.

What is one thing you like, and what is the reason?

Summary

Talking about likes is not only naming random things.

It is talking about choices in real life.

Good topics are hobbies, food taste, colors in daily life, places, mood, time, and people.

Good questions help the other person give a simple but real answer.
