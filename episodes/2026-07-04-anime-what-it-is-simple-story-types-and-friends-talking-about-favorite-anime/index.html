Anime: Stories on Screen

Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you watch cartoons or anime?

  2. Do you like funny stories, action stories, or quiet stories?

  3. Do you usually watch short videos, TV shows, or movies?

Lesson

Step 1. What Anime Is

Anime means animation from Japan. Animation is a story with moving pictures. Anime can be a TV show or a movie. Many anime stories use clear faces, strong voices, and big feelings. It does not need to be hard. You can start with one simple sentence: It is a story from Japan.

Step 2. Where Anime Stories Come From

Some anime stories first come from manga. Manga is a story in comic panels. Some anime is made first for TV or a movie. These stories are original anime. This is a small but useful anime fact. This helps you talk about the story.

Step 3. Opening Songs and Characters

Many anime episodes start with an opening song. The song can tell you the mood. The mood may be exciting, funny, warm, or quiet. Characters also matter. A kind character can make a story feel gentle. You can notice both in the first minute.

Step 4. Choosing Anime for a Friend

A good recommendation matches your friend and the time. A tired friend may want one short episode and a relaxing mood. A happy friend may want comedy. You can say the story type, one detail, and one reason. This makes your idea easy to follow.

Lesson

Lesson section

Two friends talk after class. One friend wants a simple anime to watch this weekend.

Roleplay

Dialogue Practice Role-play

Read once.

Friend A

Do you want to watch anime tonight?

Friend B

Yes, but I want something easy.

Friend A

What kind of story do you like?

Friend B

I like funny stories. I do not want a sad story today.

Friend A

Then try a comedy anime. It can be light and fun.

Friend B

Good idea. What about action anime?

Friend A

Action anime is exciting, but sometimes it is fast.

Friend B

Maybe not tonight. I am tired.

Friend A

Then maybe choose a warm school story.

Friend B

Why do you like school stories?

Friend A

I like them because the friends are kind, and the problems are small.

Friend B

That sounds relaxing.

Friend A

Start with one episode. If you like it, watch more later.

Friend B

Okay, I will try one warm story first.

Friend A

Great. Tomorrow, tell me which character you like.

Friend B

I will. Thank you for the recommendation.

Speak

Final Speaking Task

  1. Tell your partner one anime or animation you like.

  2. Say what kind of story it is.

  3. Recommend it with one reason.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Anime is animation from Japan. Some anime stories first come from manga, and many episodes start with an opening song. To recommend anime, say the story type, one detail, and one reason.
