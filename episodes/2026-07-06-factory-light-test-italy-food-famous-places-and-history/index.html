  2. Have you seen a photo of Rome, Venice, or Florence?

Lesson

Italian Food

Italy is famous for simple, tasty food. Pizza is one of the best-known Italian foods. Pasta is also very important in Italian meals. People often eat it with sauce, cheese, or vegetables. Gelato is a cold dessert like ice cream. Many people in Italy eat food slowly and enjoy time with family or friends. Word Help: Pizza is a bread food with toppings. Pasta is food made from flour and water. Gelato is soft, cold, sweet dessert.

Rome and the Colosseum

Rome is the capital of Italy. It is one of the most famous cities in the world. Rome was very important in ancient Rome, a long time ago. The Colosseum is a famous old building in Rome. People used to watch games and events there. Today, many visitors go to Rome to see its history and streets. Word Help: Rome is the capital city of Italy. Ancient Rome means Rome many centuries ago. The Colosseum is a very old, large building for public events.

Lesson section

1. What is the name of the famous old building in Rome?

Answer

Rome is the capital of Italy.

Lesson

Venice and Florence

Venice is a special city with water, canals, and bridges. People often travel by boat there. The city looks different from many other places in Italy. Florence is famous for art and beautiful old buildings. It was important during the Renaissance, a time of new ideas and art. Venice and Florence show how varied Italy can be. Word Help: Venice is a city with water roads called canals. Florence is a city known for art and old buildings. The Renaissance was a period of new art and ideas.

Check your understanding

Lesson section

1. What is one special thing about Venice?

Answer

Venice is a special city with water, canals, and bridges.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Lesson

A Short History of Italy

Italy has a very long history. Ancient Rome was a powerful part of that history. Later, the Renaissance brought new art, learning, and thinking. These two periods are very important when people study Italy. They help us understand why Italy has so many famous places and ideas. History, food, and travel all connect in Italy. Word Help: History is the study of the past. The past means time before today. A famous place is a place many people know.
