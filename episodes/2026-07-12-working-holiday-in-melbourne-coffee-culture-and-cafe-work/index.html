A2: Working Holiday in Melbourne: Coffee, Culture, and Cafe Work

Quality checked lesson

Aki is a Japanese adult on a working holiday in Melbourne. He wants a cafe job, better English, and real coffee skills.

Introduction

is a Japanese on a working holiday in Melbourne. He wants a cafe job, better English, and real coffee skills. Before his first shift, he needs to understand his visa, his workplace rights, Melbourne coffee culture, and clear cafe communication.

Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. What would you like to learn in a Melbourne cafe?

  2. What may feel different when you work in another country?

  3. Which is harder: speaking to a manager or taking a customer order?

Reading

Step 1: Check Before You Start

A working holiday lets a young adult travel and work in Australia for a time. Aki must check his own current visa conditions before he accepts a job. He should use the official Home Affairs website because conditions can change. Aki also checks the job. Is he an employee? Is it a casual job with changing hours? What is the pay, and when will he work? In Australia, migrant workers have the same workplace rights and protections as other employees. Aki should keep a record of his hours and receive a pay slip after he is paid. For exact current information, he can use the official Fair Work tools.

Check your understanding

1. What two official places can Aki check?

Answer

He can check Home Affairs for his visa and Fair Work for workplace information.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I would ask about __ first because __.

Think one step further

  • Which job detail would you ask about first?
  • Why is that detail important?
  • How can you keep a record of your hours?

Useful language

Useful Phrases

Could I check my roster and shift time? How will I receive my pay slip? Where can I check this information?

Reading

Step 2: Learn Melbourne Coffee Culture

Coffee is an important part of social life in Melbourne. Italian migration after World War II helped espresso culture grow in the city. Today, people often meet in cafes or buy coffee before work. A regular customer may visit the same cafe many times. A barista makes coffee drinks. The barista uses a grinder for the beans and steam for the milk. Milk texture means how the warm milk and foam feel. A flat white is a popular espresso-and-milk drink, but each cafe can have its own recipe and work method. Aki should watch, ask, and practise instead of guessing.

Check your understanding

1. Why should Aki learn the cafe's own method?

Answer

Cafes can make drinks and organise work in different ways.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: I would like to learn __ because __.

Think one step further

  • What coffee skill would you like to learn?
  • Why does that skill interest you?
  • How could you practise it safely?

Useful language

Useful Phrases

Could you show me how you use the grinder? How do you make a flat white here? Can I practise the milk texture again?

Reading

Step 3: Communicate on Your First Shift

On his first shift, Aki has training. Training is time to learn the job. A manager may show him the coffee machine, food-safety rules, cleaning jobs, and the order system. Required meetings and training are work time. If a cafe asks for a trial shift, Aki should check the details and use Fair Work information rather than assume it is unpaid. Australian speech can sound relaxed. Coworkers may use first names, and some people say mate. However, not everyone speaks in the same way. Friendly language does not change workplace rules. When Aki does not understand, he can clarify: ask a short question, repeat the instruction, and confirm the next action.

Check your understanding

1. What can Aki do when an instruction is not clear?

Answer

He can ask, repeat the instruction, and confirm the next action.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: A small question helps because __.

Think one step further

  • Why can a small question prevent a big mistake?
  • When is it difficult to ask for help?
  • What polite question can you use?

Useful language

Useful Phrases

Could you show me that again, please? So I clean this first, right? What should I do next?

Reading

Step 4: Check the Order and Fix Problems

Cafe English is often short and polite. Aki checks the drink, size, and milk before he starts. Then he repeats the order. This gives the customer time to correct a detail. Mistakes can still happen. Aki may hear the wrong size or use the wrong milk. He should stay calm, apologise, and explain the next action. Good service is not only fast. It is also careful and clear. A customer usually feels better when the worker notices a problem and fixes it politely.

Check your understanding

1. What three details should Aki check?

Answer

He should check the drink, size, and milk.

Look back at this section and find the main reason.

Helpful clue: The worker can say, "Sorry, __. I will __ now."

Think one step further

  • What should a worker say after a small mistake?
  • Why is a calm voice useful?
  • What action should come after the apology?

Useful language

Useful Phrases

What size would you like? Would you like regular milk or soy milk? Let me check your order. Sorry about that. I will fix it now.

Reading

Useful Speaking Patterns

- Could I check my roster and shift time? - How will I receive my pay slip? - Could you show me that again, please? - What size would you like? - Let me check your order. - Sorry about that. I will fix it now.

Story

Aki meets the manager before his first shift. He wants clear information about training, the roster, and pay.

Roleplay

Dialogue Practice Role-play

Read once.

Manager

Hi, Aki. Your training starts at nine tomorrow.

Aki

Thanks. Could I check my shift time and the rest of the roster?

Manager

Of course. I will send the roster this afternoon.

Aki

Great. How will I receive my pay slip?

Manager

It will be online. I will show you after training.

Aki

Thank you. That makes everything clear.

Manager

You're welcome. See you tomorrow.

Aki

See you tomorrow. Have a good afternoon.

Practise with the dialogue audio

Story

A customer orders a small flat white with soy milk. Aki first checks the order, then notices that he picked up regular milk.

Roleplay

Dialogue Practice Role-play

Read once.

Customer

Hi. Could I have a small flat white with soy milk?

Aki

One small flat white with soy milk. Is that right?

Customer

Yes, thanks.

Aki

Sorry, I picked up regular milk. I will remake it with soy milk now.

Customer

No problem. Thanks for checking.

Aki

Here you are. Thanks for waiting, and enjoy your coffee.

Speak

Final Speaking Task

  1. Choose one roleplay. Change one detail, such as the shift time, drink size, or milk. Practise the new conversation with a partner.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Aki can enjoy Melbourne coffee culture and protect himself at work when he checks official information, asks clear questions, and practises the cafe's method. Imagine tomorrow is your first shift abroad. What is one question you will ask your manager, and why?
