Speak

Prepare for a Real Conversation

These are preparation lines, not a roleplay. Read them aloud before you contact the company. During the real conversation, write short notes about the department, daily tasks, mentor, company relationship, and any safety information.

  1. Hello, my name is ___. My son received an internship offer from your company, and I would like to ask a few questions.

  2. Could you tell me which department he will join?

  3. What does a normal day look like for the intern?

  4. Who will be his mentor?

  5. May I confirm that Tokyo Gas is the parent company?

Vocabulary

Words for company research

oyagaisha 親会社
The main company that owns another smaller company Tokyo Gas is the parent company of Tokyo Gas Yamanashi.
kogaisha 子会社
A smaller company owned by a bigger one A subsidiary keeps its own name and staff.
gurūpu-gaisha グループ会社
A company in the same business family Tokyo Gas Yamanashi is a group company of Tokyo Gas.
kaisha gaiyō 会社概要
A short page about the company's basic facts Read the company overview page on the website.
kōshiki saito 公式サイト
The real website made by the company itself Search for the official website, not a copy.
toshi gasu 都市ガス
Gas sent to homes through pipes under the street City gas comes through pipelines.
puropan gasu プロパンガス
Gas sold in tanks, often in country areas Many houses in the countryside use LPG.
intānshippu インターンシップ
A short work experience for students The internship is two weeks long.
shidō tantō 指導担当
A staff member who guides the intern Every intern needs a mentor.
bumon 部門
One part of a company, like sales or safety Which department will he work in?
