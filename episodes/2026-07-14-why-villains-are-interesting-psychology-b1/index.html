Vocabulary

Useful Topic Vocabulary

A character who causes serious harm or works against the main character. A memorable villain gives the hero a difficult problem.
Something that may cause harm or danger. A quiet villain can feel like a bigger threat than a loud one.
The wish to learn about dark, dangerous, or frightening subjects. Morbid curiosity can make a mystery difficult to ignore.
A reason why someone does something. Iago's real motive in Othello is not fully clear.
The feeling that something is separate from your actual situation, so it feels safer to think about. Fiction creates psychological distance from real danger.
The nervous feeling of not knowing what will happen next. Iago creates suspense because the audience can see his plan.
