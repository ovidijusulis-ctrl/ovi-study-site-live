Reading

Gwangjang Market and Korean Food

Gwangjang Market is a historic market in Seoul. It has food stalls and shops for clothing and fabric.

Two foods to try are mayak gimbap and nokdujeon. Mayak gimbap are small rice rolls, and nokdujeon are savoury pancakes made from mung beans.

Another important Korean food is kimchi. Families and communities make and share kimchi in a tradition called kimjang. UNESCO added kimjang to its Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2013.

Reading

Gyeongbokgung Palace and the Subway

Gyeongbokgung was the main royal palace of the Joseon dynasty. It was established in 1395.

Its name expresses a wish for the new dynasty to enjoy great fortune and prosperity. Important cultural work took place there, including work connected to Hunminjeongeum, the original name of the Korean alphabet.

The palace was destroyed in 1592 and restored in 1867. If you travel there, check a current subway map and ask for help when you need it.

Reading

Myeong-dong Shopping

Myeong-dong is a shopping area in central Seoul. It is known for fashion and food.

The area also has Myeong-dong Cathedral and two major department stores. You can visit shops, compare clothes, and stop for something to eat.

When you buy clothes, check the label because size systems can be different. If you are not sure, ask, ‘What size is this?’ or ‘Can I try this on?’

Check: Myeong-dong Shopping

1. What is a good idea when you buy clothes while travelling?

Size systems can be different, so checking the label and trying the item on can help you choose.

You

And some water, please.

Vendor

Sure. Here you are.

You

How much is the pancake?

You

Here is 5,000 won. Thank you.

Vendor

Thank you. Enjoy your food!

Roleplay

Roleplay: Asking About the Subway

You

Excuse me. Which train should I take to Gyeongbokgung?

Worker

Take Line 3 toward Daehwa.

You

Do I need to change trains?

Worker

No. Gyeongbokgung is the next stop.

You

Which exit should I use for the palace?

Worker

Use Exit 5. The palace is about a two-minute walk.

You

Thank you. Now I know where to go.

Worker

You're welcome. Have a good trip!

Vocabulary

Key Words

market
A place where people buy and sell food or goods.Gwangjang Market has food and clothing stalls.
palace
A large building where a royal family lived.Gyeongbokgung is a palace established in 1395.
platform
The area in a station where you wait for a train.Please check the correct platform before you board.
label
A small piece of cloth or paper on an item that gives information such as its size.Check the label before you buy the jacket.
vendor
A person who sells things, often at a stall or market.The vendor took our order.
fitting room
A small room in a shop where you can try on clothes.I tried the jacket on in the fitting room.
heritage
Important traditions or buildings passed down through history.UNESCO recognised kimjang as cultural heritage in 2013.
