Reading

Part 1: Morning and School

Spain is in Europe. People there speak Spanish. They say "Hola" for "hello".

It is eight o'clock in the morning. Kids get up and wash their faces. Breakfast is small. They drink hot milk and eat toast or cookies.

School starts at nine o'clock. Kids walk to school or go by car. They carry a big school bag. In class, they read, write and do math. They learn English too.

At about eleven o'clock, kids go outside and eat a little food. A snack is a little food between meals. They often eat a sandwich. A Spanish sandwich is a "bocadillo". It is bread with ham or cheese. Then they run and play with their friends.

Reading

Part 2: A Big Lunch

Lunch is the big meal of the day in Spain. It is also late. Kids eat lunch at about two o'clock.

At many schools, classes end at two o'clock. Kids go home and eat with their family. At other schools, kids eat lunch at school. Then they have more classes until about five o'clock.

Kids eat a lot at lunch. First, they eat soup, rice or salad. Then they eat meat or fish. After that, they eat fruit.

Spain has famous food. Paella is rice with chicken or fish. Tortilla is a thick food with eggs and potatoes. Kids eat it hot or cold.

Reading

Part 3: Snack Time and Games

Dinner is very late, so kids are hungry in the afternoon. At about five or six o'clock, they eat a snack. This snack has a special name. It is "merienda". Kids eat a bocadillo, or they drink milk and eat cookies.

Then it is time for homework. After homework, kids go outside. They play in the park with their friends.

Soccer is the big sport in Spain. Real Madrid and Barcelona are famous soccer teams. Kids play soccer at school, in the park and on the street.

Many kids play basketball too. Pau Gasol is a famous basketball player from Spain. He is very tall. He played for the Los Angeles Lakers in the USA. He won the NBA two times.

Part 4: A Late Dinner

Dinner is very late in Spain. Many families eat at about nine o'clock. In Japan, dinner is usually earlier.

Dinner is a small meal. Kids eat eggs, soup or fish. The family sits at the table and talks about the day.

After dinner, kids brush their teeth and go to bed. Kids often go to bed at about ten o'clock.

Reading

Part 5: Three Kings and Summer

January 6 is a very special day for kids in Spain. It is Three Kings Day. In the story, three kings bring gifts to children.

First, kids write a letter to the kings. They ask for gifts. On the evening of January 5, the kings come to town. They walk or ride in the street with music. The kings throw candy, and kids run to get it.

That night, kids put their shoes near the door. In the morning, they run to the door. There are gifts!

Summer is another happy time. School ends in June. It starts again in September. Kids have almost three months with no school. They swim, play and visit their family.
