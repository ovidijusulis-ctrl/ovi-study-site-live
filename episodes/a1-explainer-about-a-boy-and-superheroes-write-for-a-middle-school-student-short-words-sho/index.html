Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Do you know any superheroes?

  2. What can a superhero do?

  3. Have you ever flown a kite?

Reading

Part 1: Leo loves heroes

Leo is a boy. Leo is seven years old.

Superheroes are helpers in stories. Leo loves superhero stories.

Leo reads hero books at night. Leo draws heroes with red coats.

Leo draws heroes who save people. Leo puts the drawings on his wall.

Leo wants to be a hero too. Leo thinks a hero must be big.

Leo thinks a hero must fly. Leo is small.

Leo cannot fly. Leo feels sad about that sometimes.

Reading

Part 2: The red kite goes up

Mina is Leo's little sister. Mina is four years old.

Mina has a red kite. Mina loves the red kite very much.

Leo and Mina go to the park. The wind is strong today.

The red kite goes up high. The red kite goes up and up.

The red kite gets stuck in a tall tree. Mina cries.

Mina wants her red kite. Leo looks at the tree.

The tree is very tall. Leo cannot reach the red kite. Leo cannot fly to the red kite.

Reading

Part 3: Skye comes to help

A helper walks to Leo and Mina. The helper is Skye.

Skye has a blue coat. Skye has a kind smile.

Skye does not shout. Skye does not fight.

Skye says hello to Mina in a soft voice. Skye says the red kite is safe in the tree.

Skye says they can get it down together. Skye makes a plan.

First, they need a ladder. Second, they need a grown-up.

Third, they need to stay calm. Leo listens to Skye.

Reading

Part 4: Leo is brave

Leo gets a job. Leo runs to Dad on the bench.

Leo tells Dad about the red kite. Dad brings a ladder.

Skye climbs the ladder. Leo holds the ladder.

Leo holds it very still. Mina still cries a little.

Leo holds Mina's hand. Leo tells Mina it is okay.

Skye reaches the red kite. Skye lifts it off the branch.

Skye comes down slowly. Skye gives the red kite to Mina.

Mina smiles. Mina hugs Leo.

Reading

Part 5: What Leo learns

Skye smiles at Leo. Skye says Leo was brave today.

Skye says a hero helps people. Skye says a hero stays calm.

Skye says a hero can be a kid too. Leo looks at his drawings at home.

Leo still loves big heroes. Leo also loves small help.

Leo can hold a ladder. Leo can hold a hand.

Leo can call Dad. Leo can be kind every day.

Leo feels proud. Leo is still a kid. Leo is a helper now too.
