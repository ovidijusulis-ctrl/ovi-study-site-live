Reading

Part 1: Teams and the Hoop

Basketball for kids is a team game with a ball and a hoop. Basketball is a team sport.

Two teams play. Each team has five players on the court.

The court is the place where they play. The players use a ball.

They try to shoot the ball through a hoop. The hoop is a basket on a backboard. The backboard is the board behind the basket.

Reading

Part 2: Getting Points

A made shot can give a team points. A field goal is usually worth two points.

A shot from behind the three-point line is different. It is worth three points when it goes through the hoop. The three-point line is the line on the court where a made shot is worth three points.

Reading

Part 3: The Start of Basketball

Basketball began with a gym teacher. James Naismith was the gym teacher who made basketball in 1891.

The first game was played on December 21 that year. It happened in Springfield, Massachusetts.

Springfield is the city in Massachusetts where basketball was first played. Basketball has a clear starting year, day, and place.

Part 4: A Big Basketball League

One big name in basketball is the National Basketball Association. It is a big basketball league.

People often use the short name NBA. NBA means the National Basketball Association.

When you see NBA, you now know that it names the big basketball league. This short name is also important in the story of a famous player.

Part 5: Michael Jordan and the NBA

Part 6: Wins and Nicknames
