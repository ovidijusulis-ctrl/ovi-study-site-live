Reading

Part 1: What Is Small Talk?

Daily conversation is easy talk at work or home. What people talk about in daily life is hobbies.

Small talk is short, polite talk. It helps people feel at ease.

Safe topics include weather, food, sports, and hobbies. Weekend plans are also easy to share.

Bronislaw Malinowski wrote about this kind of talk in 1923. He said it helps people stay friendly. We can notice three things: the topic, the time, and the friendly reason.

Part 2: Sports and Singing in Japan

A great player can give fans a fun topic. Karaoke is another common hobby.

Friends often rent a small karaoke box by the hour. They can sing together after work or on a weekend.

Part 3: Weekend Trips in Japan

A weekend trip can also become a talk topic. Ueno Park has over 1,000 cherry trees.

The trees bloom in late March and early April. People have a picnic under them.

This picnic is called hanami. Hot springs give people another trip idea.

Dogo Onsen is one of the oldest onsen. It is in Matsuyama. After the trip, people can share what they saw and did.

Part 4: Football Talk in Europe

Football gives many people a shared topic. The Bundesliga began in 1963.

Monday talk can follow a weekend match. FC Bayern Munich has 31 league titles.

In Spain, cafe talk often follows a big game. El Clasico is a famous football match.

Lionel Messi scored 26 goals in this match. Like baseball talk in Japan, football talk can make Monday chat easy.

Part 5: Walking and Hiking

Outdoor hobbies can give people new stories. Switzerland has more than 65,000 kilometres of marked hiking trails.

About 2.7 million people there hike often. Yellow Wanderweg signs show walking time.

Many trails go through the Alps. A Sunday hike can become a Monday story.

In Japan, a weekend onsen trip can do the same job. The place is different, but the friendly reason is the same.

Part 6: Sports and Family Time in the USA

In the USA, many people watch American football. The NFL has 32 teams.

Thanksgiving is on the fourth Thursday of November. Families often watch NFL games that weekend.

The Super Bowl is the last pro football game of the year. A listed TV record for it is 127.7 million viewers.

These events give many people the same topic at the same time. Shared time makes small talk easier.
