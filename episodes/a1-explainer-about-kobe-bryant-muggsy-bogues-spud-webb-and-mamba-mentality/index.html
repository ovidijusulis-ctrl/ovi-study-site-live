  2. Who is a person you look up to, and what do they do well?

  3. When was a time you kept trying after a hard day?

Reading

Part 1: Who Kobe Bryant was

Kobe Bryant was a famous basketball player. He was born on August 23, 1978.

He played for the Los Angeles Lakers. The Los Angeles Lakers are a team in the NBA.

The NBA is the big basketball league in the United States. Kobe Bryant played 20 years in the NBA.

He played all 20 years with the same team, the Los Angeles Lakers. That is a long time with one team.

Kobe Bryant died on January 26, 2020. Many people still remember him.

Reading

Part 2: The big wins of Kobe Bryant

Kobe Bryant won a lot. He won five NBA championships with the Los Angeles Lakers.

A championship means the team was the best team that year. In 2008, he was the NBA MVP.

MVP means the most valuable player, the best player of the season. In 1997, he won the Slam Dunk Contest.

In the Slam Dunk Contest, players jump high and try big dunks. Kobe Bryant also played for the U.S. team. He won two Olympic gold medals, in 2008 and in 2012.

Reading

Part 3: Mamba Mentality

People called Kobe Bryant the Black Mamba. A black mamba is a fast, strong snake.

The name shows how he played. From this name comes the idea of Mamba Mentality.

Mamba Mentality means work hard every day and keep trying. It is not only about game day.

It is about every practice, every morning, every small step. A student in a school club can use Mamba Mentality too.

Come to practice every day. Try again after a miss. Get a little better each time.

Reading

Part 4: Muggsy Bogues, the shortest NBA player

Basketball players are often very tall. Muggsy Bogues was not.

He was about 5 feet 3 inches tall, or 1.60 meters. He is the shortest player ever in the NBA.

He was born on January 9, 1965. He played 14 seasons in the NBA.

He played about 10 seasons with the Charlotte Hornets. He was a point guard.

A point guard starts the plays and passes the ball. Muggsy Bogues was strong at passes and steals.

He did not need height. He used speed and smart hands.

Reading

Part 5: Spud Webb and the big dunk

Spud Webb was also short. He is about 5 feet 7 inches tall, or 1.70 meters.

He was born on July 13, 1963. He was a point guard, like Muggsy Bogues.

He played for the Atlanta Hawks and other teams. In 1986, Spud Webb won the NBA Slam Dunk Contest.

A short man beat the tall players at jumping. So two short stars show two paths.

Muggsy Bogues used skill, passes, and steals. Spud Webb used big jumps and dunks.

Both worked hard every day. Both used Mamba Mentality before the name existed.
