Reading

Part 1: Two Ways to Enjoy the Story

Mob Psycho 100 first appeared as a manga, or a Japanese comic series. ONE, a Japanese artist, wrote and drew it.

The series began in 2012 and ended in 2017. ONE also made One-Punch Man.

Later, Mob Psycho 100 became an anime, or an animated series. People could read the story on a page or watch it move on a screen.

Part 2: Meet the Main Character

The main person is Shigeo Kageyama. People call him Mob.

He is a quiet boy, but he has psychic powers. Psychic powers are unusual powers of the mind.

Mob does not mainly want to be special. He wants a normal life. This difference is important: his powers are very big, but his wish is very ordinary.

Reading

Part 3: A Simple Story Pattern

Mob works with Reigen Arataka. We can understand the story with three ideas: person, wish, and problem.

The person is Mob. His wish is a normal life.

The problem is that his psychic powers make his life unusual. Reigen is part of Mob's work. This pattern helps us understand the basic story without knowing every event.
