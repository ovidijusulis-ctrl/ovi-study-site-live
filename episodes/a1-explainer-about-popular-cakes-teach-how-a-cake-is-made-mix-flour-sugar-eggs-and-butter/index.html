Reading

Part 1: How to Make a Cake

First, mix flour, sugar, eggs, and butter in a bowl. Baking powder can help the cake rise, or get bigger.

Put the mixture in a cake pan and bake it in an oven. After baking, let the cake cool, so it is not hot.

Then you can add cream, fruit, jam, or icing. People often eat cake at birthdays and weddings.

Part 2: Victoria Sponge from Britain

Victoria sponge comes from Britain, or the United Kingdom. It was named after Queen Victoria.

The cake has two soft sponge layers. Jam goes between them, like a sandwich.

Many modern cakes also have cream inside. The top is usually simple, with a little powdered sugar. Baking powder helps this buttery cake rise and become light.

Part 3: Black Forest Cake from Germany

Black Forest cake is a German layer cake. Its cake layers contain cocoa, so they are dark and taste of chocolate.

Cherries and whipped cream go between the layers. More cream, cherries, and chocolate can decorate the top.

A written recipe from 1927 is kept in Radolfzell, Germany. Today, people can find this cake in cafes around the world.

Part 4: Strawberry Shortcake in Two Countries

In the United States, strawberry shortcake has strawberries and cream with a biscuit-like shortcake or sponge cake. It is often a summer dessert.

In Japan, the cake usually has light sponge layers, whipped cream, and fresh strawberries. Fujiya introduced this style there in 1922.

It later became a popular Christmas cake. The two styles share strawberries and cream, but their cake bases can be different.

Part 5: Tres Leches Cake in Mexico

Tres leches is a Latin American cake that is very popular in Mexico. Its name means “three milks.”

Bakers soak a light sponge cake in evaporated milk, condensed milk, and whole milk. Small air bubbles in the sponge hold the milk, so the cake stays soft without becoming a wet mess. Whipped cream often goes on top, with fruit or cinnamon.
