Warm-up

  1. What pet do you think is popular near your home?

  2. What do you think makes an animal a good pet?

  3. If you could have any pet, what would you choose?

Reading

Part 1: What Is a Pet?

A pet is an animal that people keep for company or fun. Dogs and cats are very popular pets.

Fish and birds are common pets too. People may want a pet for friendship, beauty, or help protecting a home.

When we compare pets, we can ask three simple questions: Does it fit the home? How much time does it need? Why does the person want it?

Part 2: Dogs and Cats

Dogs often live closely with people. Many people enjoy playing with them and taking them for walks.

Cats also give people company, but they can be quieter at home. Cats can live indoors and usually need less outdoor walking than many dogs. For example, a busy person in an apartment may prefer a cat, while an active family may enjoy walking with a dog.

Part 3: Goldfish and Birds

A goldfish is a freshwater fish often kept in an indoor aquarium, a glass home for fish. Goldfish were first bred for color in China and later spread to other countries.

Birds can also be colorful companions. Budgies and cockatiels are popular pet birds.

People may choose birds for their company, color, or song. Both fish and birds can bring beauty into a home.

Part 4: An Unusual Pet

Some people keep hermit crabs as pets. A hermit crab can be a fun or unusual choice because it is very different from a dog, cat, fish, or bird.

Here, unusual does not mean bad. It means that fewer people first think of this animal when they hear the word “pet.” A person may choose one because they enjoy small and interesting animals.

Part 5: Pets in Different Countries

People in different countries may choose different pets because their homes, daily routines, and reasons are different. Imagine one person in a small apartment and another person in a house with more outdoor space.

The first person may choose a goldfish or cat. The second may choose a dog for walks and play. Some people want friendship, while others enjoy an animal’s beauty or want help protecting a home.

Part 6: Choosing the Right Pet

A popular pet is not always the right pet for every person. It is important to think again about home, time, and reason.

A dog may suit someone who enjoys daily walks. A cat may suit a quieter indoor home.

A goldfish needs an aquarium, and a bird may interest someone who likes color and song. Someone who wants a less common animal may think about a hermit crab.
