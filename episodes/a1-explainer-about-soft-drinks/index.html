Reading

What Is a Soft Drink?

A soft drink is a cold drink with bubbles. The bubbles come from a gas called carbon dioxide.

This gas makes the drink fizzy. Most soft drinks also have sugar and water.

Coca-Cola, for example, has water, sugar, and a special mix of flavors. It was made in Atlanta, USA, in 1886. Today, people drink about 1.9 billion servings of Coca-Cola every day.

Big Brands Around the World

Two of the biggest soft drink brands are Coca-Cola and Pepsi. Coca-Cola started in the USA.

Pepsi also started in the USA, in 1893. Both drinks are cola — dark, sweet, and fizzy.

Today, you can buy Coca-Cola in more than 200 countries. Pepsi is sold in more than 200 countries too. These brands spend billions of dollars on advertising every year.

Sugar and Health

Most soft drinks have a lot of sugar. One can of regular Coca-Cola has about 39 grams of sugar.

That is about 10 teaspoons. Doctors say too much sugar is not good for your health.

Because of this, many companies now make drinks with less sugar. Diet Coke, for example, has no sugar. In 2023, sales of low-sugar drinks grew faster than regular soft drinks in many countries.

New Flavors and Trends

Soft drink companies always try new flavors. In 2023, Coca-Cola released a special flavor called Coca-Cola Starlight.

It was only available for a short time. Pepsi also makes many different flavors, like Pepsi Mango and Pepsi Lime.

In Japan, Fanta makes unusual flavors like melon and grape. New flavors help companies attract young people and stay popular.

Soft Drinks and the Environment

Soft drinks come in cans and plastic bottles. These can create a lot of waste.

Many countries now ask people to recycle cans and bottles. Coca-Cola said in 2022 that it wants all its packaging to be recyclable by 2025.

Plastic bottles are a big problem for the ocean. Some companies now use recycled plastic to make new bottles.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Soft drinks are popular around the world. They are fizzy because of carbon dioxide. Big brands like Coca-Cola and Pepsi sell their drinks in more than 200 countries. Many people are now choosing drinks with less sugar. Companies also try new flavors and think about the environment.

What do you think? Are soft drinks a good thing or a bad thing? Why?
