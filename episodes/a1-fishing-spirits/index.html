Introduction

Fishing Spirits is a video game about getting fish. You can play on a big machine or at home.

First, we will see how it started and why it is fun. Then we will meet the fish you can keep.

Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Do you like games about animals?

  2. Do you play games with your friends?

  3. Do you like looking at fish?

Reading

Part 1: From machines to home

Fishing Spirits started in Japan in November 2012. In 2015, the machines came to other countries as Ace Angler.

You stand around the machine with other people. You all see fish in the same water on the screen. This is where the machine shows the game.

In Japan, the first home game came out in July 2019. You play it on Nintendo Switch. This game was popular. People bought more than 850,000 copies. A copy is one game.

Another home game is Ace Angler: Fishing Spirits. It came out in the United States on 28 October 2022. It is from Bandai Namco Entertainment, a game company. These people make and sell games. You play it on Nintendo Switch. This small game machine is for playing by yourself or with friends.

1. What is the name of the big game outside Japan?

Sample answer

Reading

Part 2: Your hands do the work

You hold a fishing rod, a long thing for getting fish. Move it to put your line in the water. The line goes from the thing you hold to the fish.

A fish takes the food. Turn the reel, a small thing by your hand. This brings the fish to you.

The thing you hold moves when the fish takes the food. The fish pulls in the game. This makes the reel hard to turn. Your hands feel this, so getting a fish feels like work.

You can give the fish an electric shock with a button. Electricity hits the fish in the game. Electricity is what makes lights work. The shock makes the fish weaker, so it is easier to get. Each time, you use one point.

The game is easy to learn. You move your hands to play. It feels like getting a real fish.

1. Why is the reel not easy to turn?

Sample answer

Reading

Part 3: Big fish

At first, you see dark fish in the water. You cannot see which fish it is yet. It takes the food. Then you see which fish it is.

You get points when you get a fish. These are numbers you win in the game. A fish that is hard to get gives you more.

There are three places with different fish. You see new fish when the game moves to another place. You can get a small fish or a very big one.

Reading

Part 4: More fun at home

In the home game, you get medals when you play. These are small things you use like money in the game.

You use them in another game machine to get new fish. You do not choose which fish you get. These fish go into your aquarium. This is a home for fish that people can look at.

There are more than 250 kinds of fish to get. A kind is a group of the same fish. You can watch the fish and learn about them. You can get a shark, too. This is a big sea fish with many teeth.

There are short games for your family and friends, too. You can also play with people who are not near you. In the story, you help make dirty seas clean again. You play to help the fish have clean water.
