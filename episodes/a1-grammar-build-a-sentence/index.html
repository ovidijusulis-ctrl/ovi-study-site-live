Reading

Part 1: Am, Is and Are

You meet Emma and Ben. Be tells a name, job, place or feeling.

PersonBeShort form
IamI'm
he, she, itishe's, she's, it's
you, we, theyareyou're, we're, they're

Rule: Match am, is or are to the person.

For a yes/no question, put be first. For a negative, add not after be. The word you can mean one person or a group.

Use it for weather and time: It's sunny. It's three o'clock.

  • Statement: Ben is at school.
  • Question: Is Ben at school? Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
  • Negative: Ben is not at school.
  • Emma is a teacher. Is she a teacher? Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
  • They are at home. Are they at home? Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
  • Ben, are you ready? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Class, are you ready? Yes, we are.

Watch out: Not Do you are ready? Say Are you ready?

    Part 2: English Sentence Order

    Ben and Emma talk in class.

    Person or thingVerbOther words
    Weread–
    Wereadbooks at home
    Heishappy

    Rule: In a statement, the person or thing comes before the verb.

    A question word comes first, before be. Where asks about a place. What asks about a thing.

    • We read. We = subject. Read = verb. It is a complete sentence.
    • They play football. They = subject. Play = verb.
    • He is happy. This sentence needs happy. We read books at home. You can add these words.
    • Where is the bag? It's on the chair. / What is your name? My name is Ben.

    Watch out: Not Where the bag is? Say Where is the bag?

    Study: Compare Emma is at school. and Is Emma at school? What changes?

      Part 3: Be or an Action Verb

      Ben is at the door. Be tells a feeling or a place. An action verb shows an action, like walk.

      BeAction verb
      I am ready.I walk to school.
      They are at home.They read books.

      Rule: Never put be just before a verb like go.

      • I am happy.
      • She is tired.
      • We play football after school.
      • They read at home.

      Watch out: Not I am go to school. Go is the verb. Am is extra. Say I go to school.
