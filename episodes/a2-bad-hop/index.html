  1. What music do you listen to?

  2. Do you prefer one rapper or a group?

  3. Do you watch music videos?

Reading

Part 1: A Crew of Friends

BAD HOP were a hip-hop crew of eight rappers from Kawasaki. A crew is a group of people who work together. Rappers speak words with the beat of the music.

They started the group in 2014. They were friends long before they became a music group.

Twin brothers T-Pablow and YZERR were at the centre of the group. Twins are two children born to the same mother at the same time. The brothers helped lead the crew and make its music.

Reading

Part 2: Growing Up with Rap

The friends grew up in a difficult part of Kawasaki. Money was a problem in their area. Rap gave them a way to tell people about their lives.

T-Pablow started rapping as a teenager. In 2012, he won the BAZOOKA!!! High School Rap Championship. It was a rap battle contest for young rappers.

In a rap battle, people take turns rapping against each other. They make up their words as they rap. The words are not from a song they learned before.

Reading

Part 3: Doing It Themselves

BAD HOP worked independently, without a big record company behind them. Here, independent means doing the work and making the decisions yourself. They had to do more than write songs.

In 2018, they gave a concert at Nippon Budokan in Tokyo. A concert is a show where people perform music for other people. All the tickets sold.

They organised the concert themselves and paid its costs. That meant finding the money to put the show on. Their work included the business side of music.

Reading

Part 4: The Final Concert

BAD HOP gave their final concert at Tokyo Dome in February 2024. They were the first Japanese hip-hop group to headline their own concert there. Headline means being the main act at a show.

The group ended after that night. Fans heard old songs and new music during the concert. The music brought parts of their story together in one show.

Later, YZERR released new music under his own name. He continued his story as a rapper after the group ended.

Useful phrases

I got into their music through ...
explain how your interest startedI got into their music through a friend's playlist.
I like the way they ...
explain what you enjoy about their musicI like the way they tell stories about their lives.
I'd love to see them live.
talk about a concert you want to attendI'd love to see YZERR live because his songs give me energy.
