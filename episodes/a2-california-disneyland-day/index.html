Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Have you been to a theme park before? What did you enjoy most?

  2. Before a big day out, how do you get tickets? Do you buy them at the gate or online?

  3. At a busy place, what do you do when you need help? Do you ask a person or use your phone?

Reading

Part 1: The Resort and Your Ticket

Disneyland in California is not just one park. It is a big resort with many parts. It has two parks, Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure. It also has Downtown Disney, a shopping and dining area. There are three hotels too. They are the Disneyland Hotel, Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa and Pixar Place Hotel. Pixar Place Hotel got its new name in 2024. Before your day, decide which park you want to visit.

Tickets work differently now than in the past. Before 1982, guests paid to go in. Then they paid again for each ride with special tickets. These ride tickets had letters, from A to C. Since 1982, one admission price lets you use most attractions as often as you like. This makes the day simpler. You pay once and then you ride.

Where should you get your ticket? You can get park tickets from the official Disneyland Resort website. This is a safe choice because it comes straight from Disney.

Some rides have long lines. The normal line is called the standby line. Disney sells Lightning Lane passes. With these, you can skip the standby line at some rides, so you save time. Not every ride has this. Prices can change, so do not guess. Check the official website before you buy.

Check your understanding

Check: Part 1: The Resort and Your Ticket

1. Why is the ticket system easier today than before 1982?

Sample answer

Before 1982, guests paid to enter and then paid again for each ride with A to C tickets. Since 1982, one admission price lets you use most attractions as many times as you want. So you only pay once, and you do not need to buy tickets for each ride.

Useful language

Useful phrases

Is this the way into Disneyland Park?
Use it when you are not sure you are at the right entrance.Excuse me, is this the way into Disneyland Park?
I bought it on the official Disneyland website.
Use it to explain where your ticket came from.My ticket is on my phone. I bought it on the official Disneyland website.
How much does it cost?
Use it to ask a price before you decide to buy.Lightning Lane sounds good. How much does it cost?
Here you go.
Say it when you hand or show something to someone.Here you go. This is my ticket.

Reading

Part 2: Getting Help With a Table

Inside the park, the staff can help you with many things. A common question is about food. Some restaurants are table-service restaurants. This means you sit down, and a server brings your food. These places can be busy. So how do you get a table?

The Disneyland app can help. At some table-service restaurants, you can add your group to a walk-up list. You can do this only if the restaurant has space. You can check if there is space in two ways. You can look in the app, or you can scan a QR code near the restaurant entrance. You do not need to go to the host stand. The host stand is the desk at the front, where staff welcome guests.

This is helpful for food needs too. When you add your group to the list, you can confirm details in the app. You can also tell the restaurant about special dietary needs. For example, maybe someone in your group cannot eat eggs. You can write this in the app. You do not need to explain it out loud in a busy place.

Check: Part 2: Getting Help With a Table

1. Your friend cannot eat eggs, and you want a table at a busy restaurant. What can you do with the Disneyland app?

Sample answer

First, check in the app, or scan the QR code near the entrance, to see if the restaurant has space. If it has space, add your group to the walk-up list. When you add your group, you can confirm the details and tell the restaurant that your friend cannot eat eggs.

Useful language

Useful phrases

Could you help me?
Use it to start a polite request for help.Excuse me, sorry to bother you. Could you help me?
How do we get a table?
Use it to ask how to eat at a sit-down restaurant.We want to eat here later. How do we get a table?
Do I need to go to the restaurant first?
Use it to check if you must go somewhere in person.Do I need to go to the restaurant first, or can I use my phone?
my friend can't eat eggs
Use it to explain a food need for someone in your group.Also, my friend can't eat eggs. Is that a problem?
Thank you for your help!
Say it to end a conversation with a helpful person.That's very clear. Thank you for your help!

Part 3: Ordering Food With the App

What if the restaurant has no space? Or what if you just want a quick meal? You can use the Disneyland app to order food and drinks ahead of time. This works at some dining places in the park.

Here is how it works, step by step. First, you choose a restaurant. Next, you pick an arrival time. This is the time you plan to come and get your food. Then you customize your order. That means you can change it. For example, you can take out something you do not want. Last, you pay. There are several ways to pay, including a Disney Gift Card and Apple Pay.

The app can do more outside the parks too. If you stay at a Disneyland hotel, you can order meals and snacks in the app. Then you pick them up at some hotel locations. You can also choose some Downtown Disney restaurants and see their food. Some of them can deliver your order to a hotel lobby.

Check: Part 3: Ordering Food With the App

1. Explain to a friend how to order lunch in the Disneyland app, from start to finish.

Sample answer

First, choose a restaurant. Next, pick an arrival time, which is when you will come to get the food. Then customize the order, for example by taking out something you cannot eat. Finally, pay with one of the payment methods, such as a Disney Gift Card or Apple Pay.

Useful language

Useful phrases

Let's order food in the app.
Use it to suggest the app when lines are long or there is no table.There's no space right now. Let's order food in the app.
What would you like?
Use it to ask someone what they want to eat or drink.I'm ordering now. What would you like?
But it can't have eggs.
Use it to say what must not be in the food.I'll have this one. But it can't have eggs.
I'd like the same
Use it when you want the same order as someone else.I'd like the same, but with a bigger drink.
How do we pay?
Use it to ask about payment methods.The order looks good. How do we pay?

You

Hi! Good morning. Is this the way into Disneyland Park?

Daniel (park staff)

Good morning! Yes, it is. Welcome! Do you have your ticket?

You

Yes. I bought it on the official Disneyland website. It's on my phone.

Daniel (park staff)

Perfect. That's a great place to get tickets. Is this your first visit?

You

Yes, it's my first time. I came from Japan. I'm so excited!

Daniel (park staff)

Oh, welcome! That's a long trip. How was your flight?

You

Long, but okay. I slept a little. Can I ask something? My ticket is for Disneyland Park. Can I also go to Disney California Adventure today?

Daniel (park staff)

Good question. They are two different parks. Please look at your ticket details on your phone or on the official website. They show which park you can visit.

You

Okay, I'll check. One more question. What is Lightning Lane?

Daniel (park staff)

Lightning Lane passes let you skip the standby line at some rides. Standby is the normal line. So you save time.

You

That sounds nice. How much does it cost?

Daniel (park staff)

The price can change, so I don't want to guess. Please check the official Disneyland website before you buy.

You

Got it. I'll look at it in a minute.

Daniel (park staff)

Great. Can I see your ticket, please?

You

Here you go.

Daniel (park staff)

Thank you. You're all set. Have a magical day!

You

Thank you so much! See you later.

You

Rosa (park staff)

Of course! What do you need?

You

Some table-service restaurants have a walk-up list in the Disneyland app. If there's space, you can add your group.

Do I need to go to the restaurant first?

Not always. You can check in the app. Or you can scan the QR code near the restaurant entrance. You don't need to go to the host stand.

You

Sorry, what's a host stand?

Rosa (park staff)

It's the desk at the front, where staff welcome guests.

You

I see. Also, my friend can't eat eggs. Is that a problem?

Rosa (park staff)

I'm glad you asked. When you add your group to the list, you can tell them about special dietary needs in the app.

You

That's a relief. Last year, at a restaurant in [your city], it was hard to explain.

Oh, I understand. Writing it in the app makes it easier.

And what if there's no space?

Rosa (park staff)

Then you can check again later. Or you can order food ahead of time in the app at some quick places.

You

Great. Thank you for your help!

You're welcome. Enjoy your lunch!

Speak

Speaking

Work with a partner. Plan a different day at the Disneyland Resort. First, choose one park: Disneyland Park or Disney California Adventure. Then do three short roleplays.

1. One person is the guest and one is park staff at the entrance. The guest shows their ticket and asks about Lightning Lane. The staff explains it and says how to find the price.

2. The guest asks staff for help getting a table. The guest has a food need, for example no nuts or no meat. The staff explains the walk-up list and the QR code.

3. Two friends order lunch in the app. Go through every step: choose a restaurant, pick an arrival time, customize the food, and pay.

Use at least five phrases from this lesson. Then change roles and do it again.
