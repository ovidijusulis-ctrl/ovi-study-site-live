Central Cee from West London to Number One

Quality checked lesson

Published 10 Nov 2026

Central Cee grew up in west London, found grime through his friends, played a show close to home in 2021, and reached number one with the song Sprinter and his first studio album.

Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. What is your favorite song?

  2. Where do you listen to music?

  3. Do you know any rappers?

Reading

Part 1: A rapper from West London

Central Cee is a rapper from west London. A rapper says words to the beat of the music. Central Cee grew up in Shepherd's Bush, a part of west London. When he was young, he listened to music with his dad. His dad played hip-hop and reggae.

He also found new music through his friends. His dad did not play much music from the UK. His friends played him a UK kind of music called grime. He loved it.

Part 2: A show close to home

As a teenager, he went into a music studio for the first time. A studio is a room where people make music. A friend from school took him there, and he made his first song. That visit helped him see what he could do with music.

In 2021, Central Cee played a show in Shepherd's Bush. About two thousand fans came to see him. This was the part of London where he grew up. He was back in a place he knew well. The fans were there to hear his music.

Part 3: A song with Dave

Dave is a rapper, too. He and Central Cee made a song together. Sprinter came out in 2023. It reached number one on the UK song chart.

A song chart is a list of popular songs. The order can change every week. Number one is the top song on the list. Sprinter stayed at the top for ten weeks. That is more than two months at number one.

Reading

Part 4: His first studio album

An album is a group of songs that come out together. In 2025, Central Cee put out his first studio album. A studio album is made in a studio. His album is called Can't Rush Greatness. To rush means to try to do something too fast.

His album reached number one on the UK album chart. So a song and an album both reached the top.

Sources and fact review (6)
  1. Central Cee: Brave new world (Crack Magazine, 17 March 2021)

    He grew up in Shepherd's Bush, west London; in 2013, as a teenager, a friend from school took him to a studio, where he made his first song, and this showed him he could achieve something with music

  2. I Don't Want To Be Boxed In As A Drill Rapper: An Interview With Central Cee (Complex, 12 March 2021)

    His dad played hip-hop and reggae but not a lot of UK music; his friends introduced him to grime and he loved it

  3. Central Cee, GQ Men of the Year 2024 interview (British GQ, 2024)

    In 2021 he played the O2 Shepherd's Bush Empire, in the area where he grew up, for 2,000 fans

  4. Sprinter by Dave and Central Cee (Official Charts)

    Sprinter came out in 2023, reached number 1 on the Official Singles Chart and spent 10 weeks at number 1

  5. Central Cee secures biggest opening week of his career with CAN'T RUSH GREATNESS (Official Charts, 31 January 2025)

    His first studio album came out in 2025, reached number 1 on the Official Albums Chart and had the biggest opening week of his career
