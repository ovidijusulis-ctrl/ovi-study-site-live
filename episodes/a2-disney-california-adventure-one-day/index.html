Introduction

Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Have you ever been to a theme park? What did you do first?

  2. When you travel with a child, how do you choose what to do?

  3. Do you like big, fast rides, or do you prefer shows and food?

Reading

Part 1: 8:00 a.m. — Through the Gate to Buena Vista Street

It is 8:00 a.m. You and Hana walk up to the main gate. Before you go in, look up. The gate building has a big, rounded shape. It looks like the Pan-Pacific Auditorium, a famous old building.

When the park rebuilt this area, it moved the main gate further out. So the gate now stands in front of a whole new street. That street is Buena Vista Street, and it is the first thing you see inside.

Hana points to a red car moving slowly down the street. This is the Red Car Trolley, and it runs along Buena Vista Street. She wants to ride it right away. You smile and say, "Later." First, you want to plan the rides while the morning is still quiet.

Check your understanding

Check: Part 1: 8:00 a.m. — Through the Gate to Buena Vista Street

1. What does the main gate look like, and what does Hana see on Buena Vista Street?

Sample answer

The main gate looks like the Pan-Pacific Auditorium, a famous old building. On Buena Vista Street, Hana sees the Red Car Trolley, which runs along the street.

Reading

Part 2: 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. — Three Rides and Three Height Questions

At 9:00 a.m., you walk into Cars Land. Radiator Springs Racers comes from the 2006 film Cars. Six people sit in one car. Your car races another car, and the winner is random. You cannot plan to win. The ride was also very expensive to build. It is the most expensive attraction at Disneyland Resort and one of the most expensive in the world. Before you join the line, check the height sign at the entrance or in the app. Hana stands next to it to make sure.

At 10:30 a.m., you reach Guardians of the Galaxy – Mission: Breakout! Riders must be at least 40 inches (102 cm) tall. But the park warns that this ride has big drops, and it is loud and scary. The park says it is best for teens and adults. So you must decide as her parent.

Next comes the Incredicoaster, a fast steel roller coaster. It launches forward instead of slowly climbing up. It is the only coaster at Disneyland Resort that turns riders upside down. It is also the fastest, at 55 mph (about 89 km/h). Riders must be at least 48 inches (122 cm) tall.

Lines can be long at busy rides. You may also hear the name Lightning Lane. Before you pay for anything, open the app. Read what Lightning Lane offers and what it costs on that day.

Check: Part 2: 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. — Three Rides and Three Height Questions

1. Which of the two rides with known height limits can Hana ride, and why?

Sample answer

Hana can ride Guardians of the Galaxy – Mission: Breakout! because she is 115 cm and the limit is 102 cm. However, it is loud and scary, so you should still decide whether it is right for her. She cannot ride the Incredicoaster because the limit is 122 cm, and she is about 7 cm too short.

Useful language

Useful phrases

is my daughter tall enough for this ride?
Ask a staff member before you wait in a long line.Excuse me, is my daughter tall enough for this ride?
How scary is it?
Ask this when a ride might be too much for a child.She loves fast rides, but how scary is it?
How long is the wait right now?
Ask about the line before you join it.Hi, how long is the wait right now?
Thanks for telling me before we wait.
Thank someone who saves you from a wasted wait.Oh, she's too short. Thanks for telling me before we wait.

Reading

Part 3: 12:30 p.m. — Lunch at Corn Dog Castle

You go to Corn Dog Castle, a snack stand. Its cheese stick is one of the park's most popular snacks.

The park does not print one price for everyone online, so look first. Before you order, check the price on the menu board or in the Disneyland app. You can also order and pay for food in the app. That can save time when the stand is busy. Today you order at the counter so Hana can watch.

There are bigger meals in the park too. Some guests make dining reservations for a sit-down lunch. But for a fast snack between rides, a stand like Corn Dog Castle works well.

Check: Part 3: 12:30 p.m. — Lunch at Corn Dog Castle

1. How can you find the price of the cheese stick before you order, and how can you pay?

Sample answer

You can find the price on the menu board at the stand or in the Disneyland app. You can pay at the counter, or you can order and pay in the app.

Reading

Part 4: 3:00 p.m. — Choosing Mickey Ears

At 3:00 p.m., you walk into a gift shop. Hana goes straight to the ear headbands. These are the famous Mickey and Minnie ears. A news report from October 2026 says these headbands now cost about $38.99. Before the price increase, they cost $36.99.

That is not cheap, so you think before you buy. One headband costs about $38.99. Two headbands cost about $77.98. Maybe Hana gets one pair, and you take photos of her wearing them. For a small gift for her friend at home, look at the price tag on each item. Then compare before you choose.

Check: Part 4: 3:00 p.m. — Choosing Mickey Ears

1. Why do you think carefully before you buy two headbands?

Sample answer

Each headband costs about $38.99 after a price increase from $36.99. So two headbands cost about $77.98. That is a lot of money, so you compare before you choose.

Reading

Part 5: Evening — World of Color and the Walk Out

The park has character meet and greets with Mickey and others, along with parades and shows. Before the show, Hana meets Mickey. She shows him her new ears.

The big evening show is World of Color. The time can change from day to day, so check it in the Disneyland app. Then find your place early. Hana sits on your lap, tired but excited.

When the show ends, you walk back to the gate. Buena Vista Street is lit up at night, and Hana is quiet now. You pass the spot where the Red Car Trolley runs. You walk out under the big, rounded gate. She is holding your hand again, just like this morning.

You

Excuse me, is my daughter tall enough for this ride?

Jordan

Let's check. Hana, can you stand next to this sign? You need to be 40 inches, or 102 centimetres.

Hana

I'm 115 centimetres!

Jordan

Then you're tall enough. Nice! Just so you know, there are big drops, and it's very loud.

You

How scary is it? She's only seven.

Jordan

The park says it's best for teens and adults. Some younger kids love it, and some get scared. You know her best.

You

Hana, do you still want to go?

Hana

Yes! I want to scream!

You

OK, she's ready. How long is the wait right now?

Jordan

The wait time is on the sign by the entrance, and the Disneyland app shows it too. It changes during the day.

You

Thanks. And after this, we want to try the Incredicoaster.

Jordan

For that one, riders need to be 48 inches, or 122 centimetres. So Hana can't ride it yet.

You

Oh, she'll be sad. Thanks for telling me before we wait.

Jordan

No problem. She'll be tall enough in a few years. Have fun in there!

Speaking

Work with a partner. Tell the story of Hana's day in time order, from the morning to the night. Explain which rides Hana could ride and why. Then change roles. Your partner is a Cast Member, and you ask about one ride, one snack and one souvenir.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Hana's height decided many choices today, from Guardians to the Incredicoaster. The Disneyland app helped you check wait times, prices and show times before you decided. At night, you left through the same gate where your day began.

  1. Would you let a seven-year-old ride a loud, scary ride if she was tall enough? Why?

  2. Is $38.99 a fair price for a souvenir like Mickey ears?

  3. Which part of this day would you enjoy most, and which part would be hardest?
