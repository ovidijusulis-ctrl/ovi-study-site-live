Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Do you know any Japanese manga or anime? Which one do you like?

  2. What do you do when a brother, sister, or friend is in trouble?

  3. Do you like stories with monsters, or do you prefer quiet stories about daily life?

Reading

Part 1: What Demon Slayer is

Demon Slayer is a Japanese manga. Its Japanese title is Kimetsu no Yaiba. The artist and writer is Koyoharu Gotouge. The manga ran in the magazine Weekly Shōnen Jump from February 2016 to May 2020, and the full story fills 23 volumes. In 2019, the studio Ufotable made an anime, and the anime made the story famous all over the world.

The story happens in Japan in the Taisho era. That is the time from 1912 to 1926, when Japan had trains and electric lights in cities, but many villages in the mountains still lived in the old way. In this world, demons hide in the dark and eat people. A small group of sword fighters, the Demon Slayer Corps, hunts them at night. The story follows one boy who joins this group.

Reading

Part 2: Tanjiro and Nezuko

Tanjiro Kamado is the main character. He is a kind, hard-working boy who lives in the mountains with his mother, brothers, and sisters. His father is dead, so Tanjiro sells charcoal in the town below to feed the family. He has a very strong nose, and he can smell feelings and danger.

One day he goes to town and stays the night there because of the snow. When he comes home the next morning, a demon has killed his whole family. Only his younger sister, Nezuko Kamado, is alive, and the demon's blood has turned her into a demon too. Most demons attack humans right away, but Nezuko does not. She still loves her brother and protects him. Tanjiro now has one clear goal for the whole story. He wants to find a cure and make Nezuko human again.

Sample answer

A demon kills the whole family while Tanjiro is in town. Nezuko becomes a demon, but she does not attack humans and still protects her brother.

Think one step further

Reading

Part 3: Training and the Demon Slayer Corps

On the same morning, a sword fighter named Giyu Tomioka finds Tanjiro and Nezuko. He is a member of the Demon Slayer Corps, and his job is to kill demons. He sees that Nezuko protects her brother instead of eating him, so he lets her live. He sends Tanjiro to an old teacher, Sakonji Urokodaki, who lives on a mountain.

Tanjiro trains there for two years. He runs down the mountain at night, jumps over traps, and cuts a huge rock with his sword. He learns Water Breathing. This is a way of breathing and moving that makes a sword fighter strong and fast, and each move looks like flowing water. In the end, he passes the Final Selection, a seven-day test on a mountain full of demons. He becomes a Demon Slayer, and Nezuko travels with him in a wooden box on his back, because sunlight can kill a demon.

Reading

Part 4: Friends on the road

Tanjiro does not fight alone for long. On his first missions, he meets two other young Demon Slayers, and they become his team. Zenitsu Agatsuma is a boy with yellow hair who is often afraid and cries a lot. But he has very good ears, and when he sleeps, his body fights with Thunder Breathing, a fast style like lightning. He also likes Nezuko very much.

Inosuke Hashibira is very different. Wild boars raised him in the mountains, so he wears a boar head as a mask and shouts all the time. He fights with two swords and calls his style Beast Breathing. He loves to fight and wants to be the strongest. The three boys are noisy and often argue, but they help each other in every hard battle. They work together, and teamwork becomes a big theme of the story.

Reading

Part 5: Muzan Kibutsuji and the demons

Every demon in the story comes from one person. Muzan Kibutsuji is the first demon, and he is more than one thousand years old. Long ago, a doctor tried to cure his illness, and the medicine changed him into a demon. Now his blood can turn humans into demons too. Muzan killed Tanjiro's family, so he is also Tanjiro's personal enemy.

Demons are strong, they heal fast, and they eat humans to grow stronger. But they cannot go into sunlight, and a special sword to the neck can kill them. Muzan hates the sun. His dream is to become a perfect being who can walk in the day. He hides in cities, sometimes looking like a rich man with a family, and he sends his twelve strongest demons to fight for him. The Demon Slayer Corps has hunted him for a very long time. Tanjiro also meets Tamayo, a demon doctor who does not eat humans, and she agrees to help him look for Nezuko's cure.

Reading

Part 6: The long road and the big goal

Tanjiro's road is long. He and his friends fight many strong demons on their missions. Some demons are lonely. Some are cruel. Each fight teaches Tanjiro something new about breathing, swords, and people. Above the normal Demon Slayers stand the Hashira. The Hashira are the strongest sword fighters in the Demon Slayer Corps, and each one has a special breathing style. Tanjiro meets several of them as the story goes on, and he learns how hard their job is.

Through every battle, Tanjiro keeps two goals. He wants to make Nezuko human again, and he wants to stop Muzan Kibutsuji so demons stop hurting families. The story is not only about fighting. It is also about kindness, family, and friends who stay together when the night is long. That is why so many readers and viewers care about Tanjiro, Nezuko, Zenitsu, and Inosuke.
