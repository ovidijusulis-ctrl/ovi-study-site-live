Reading

Part 1: What kind of game is it?

Elden Ring is a 2022 action RPG. In this kind of game, you control a character, explore, fight, and become stronger.

FromSoftware, a Tokyo game studio, led its creation. Bandai Namco published the game outside Japan, while FromSoftware published it in Japan.

Hidetaka Miyazaki, the director of Elden Ring, directed the project. The game first came out on 25 February 2022. People could play it on PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Windows, Xbox One, and Xbox Series X/S.

Part 2: Exploring the world

The Lands Between is the world of Elden Ring. It is an open world, so players have a lot of room to wander and choose where to go.

You play as a Tarnished, the kind of character the player is. This character is an exile who is called back to repair the Elden Ring and become Elden Lord.

Travel is important because the world is very large. You can ride a horse named Torrent. You also meet enemies and fight them with different weapons or with magic.

Part 3: Ideas from earlier games

The game’s roots reach back to Demon's Souls, an earlier game that started this style. It came out in 2009 and made hard fights and death part of learning.

Dark Souls, an earlier FromSoftware hard action game, followed in 2011. Its areas used narrower paths, and players had to put parts of the story together.

Production of Elden Ring began in early 2017, after work on extra content for Dark Souls III. The studio wanted to keep this difficult style but place it in a bigger open world with more freedom to wander.

1. What important change did FromSoftware want for Elden Ring?

Sample answer

FromSoftware wanted a bigger open world with more room to wander than the narrower paths in Dark Souls.

Reading

Part 4: The director’s role

Hidetaka Miyazaki joined FromSoftware in 2004, when he was 29. He first worked as a game planner.

Later, he took over Demon's Souls when the project was in trouble and helped shape the finished game. He then directed Dark Souls and became president of FromSoftware in 2014.

In his games, the story often does not arrive through many long movie scenes. Players find parts of it in short item texts and in the places they visit. This style asks players to look carefully and connect small pieces of information.

Sample answer

Reading

Part 5: Two kinds of writing

George R. R. Martin, the writer of the mythic background for the world, had a special job. Hidetaka Miyazaki asked him to create the old background of the world.

This gave the team a history behind the people and events in the game. However, Martin did not design the fights or the open world.

He also did not write the item texts that players read. FromSoftware built the game and its play. Miyazaki stayed the lead writer for the story that players meet during their journey.

Sample answer

Reading

Part 6: From announcement to success

Elden Ring was shown to the public at E3 in June 2019 and was released in February 2022. Later that year, it received major prizes.

The Game Awards is a yearly show that names the year’s best games. On 8 December 2022, Elden Ring won Game of the Year there.

It also won Best Game Direction, Best Art Direction, and Best Role Playing Game that night. Its audience continued to grow after release. By April 2025, the game had sold more than 30 million copies.
