Reading

Part 1: Meet Sonic

**Sonic the Hedgehog** is a fast platform game from 1991. A platform game asks you to move and jump across places on the screen. It is also side-scrolling. This means the view moves sideways as you travel through the level.

You control a blue hedgehog who runs at high speed. You collect rings and try to stop **Doctor Robotnik**, the main enemy. **Doctor Eggman** is this enemy’s name in Japan. The blue hedgehog is also the famous mascot of **Sega**, a Japanese video game company. A mascot is a character that represents a company.

Reading

Part 2: Run, Jump, and Collect Rings

You run and jump to move through the game. Jumping also lets Sonic attack. He curls into a ball and spins. He can roll into a ball on the ground, too. This move is called a **Spin Attack**. It helps him defeat enemies.

The gold circles you collect are called **rings**. They help protect Sonic. When an enemy hits him, he drops his rings instead of losing a life. You can try to collect some again. With no rings, getting hit by an enemy can cost him a life. Speed helps, but rings matter too.

Part 3: Hills, Loops, and Bosses

The game has different areas called zones. **Green Hill Zone** is the famous first level. It has green hills and loops. A loop is a path that goes around in a circle. Sonic can run through these loops at high speed.

Later zones give you different places to explore. At the end of many zones, you meet a boss. A boss is a strong enemy in a special fight. You need to watch its movements and attack carefully. There are also **Chaos Emeralds**, special gems you can collect in special stages outside the main zones.

Part 4: The Game’s Start in 1991

**Sonic Team**, a game-making team at Sega, developed the original game. Sega published it. It came out for the **Sega Genesis**, a home game console. **Mega Drive** is the console’s name outside North America.

The game arrived in North America and PAL regions, including Europe, in June 1991. It arrived in Japan on July 26, 1991. In the United States, Sega included the Genesis as pack-in with the console. This pack-in copy helped many people start with Sonic. Including those pack-in copies, it sold over 15 million copies on the Sega Genesis. This first game started a huge franchise, a family of related games and other products.

Part 5: The Famous Original

The original 1991 game is the famous first classic at the center of Sonic’s story. When people talk about classic Sonic, they often mean this game. Its first green level, gold rings, and fast movement are important parts of that picture.

These parts work together. Speed makes running exciting. Rings give you a reason to look around and collect things. Hills and loops make the path more interesting than a straight road. You do not only run as fast as possible. You also choose when to jump, when to collect rings, and when to slow down.

Part 6: One Game Became Many

**Sonic the Hedgehog 2** came out in 1992. Many more games followed. Together, they became a large series. Sonic grew from the star of one game into a character known far beyond that first release.

Still, the 1991 original is the famous first flagship. A flagship is a leading product that helps people know a company. You do not need to know every later game to understand this classic. Start with its main ideas: a blue hedgehog, fast movement, jumping, rings, and an enemy to stop. That gives you a clear picture of where the series began.
