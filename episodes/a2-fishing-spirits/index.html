Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Have you ever been fishing, or have you watched someone fish? What was it like?

  2. Do you like games that you play with your body, not only with buttons?

  3. Why do some people enjoy collecting things, like cards, stamps or photos?

Reading

Part 1: One Series, Two Names

Fishing Spirits is a series of fishing video games from the Japanese company Bandai Namco. In Japan, the name is Tsuri Spirits. “Tsuri” is the Japanese word for “fishing.” Outside Japan, the series has a different name: Ace Angler. An “angler” is a person who fishes with a long stick and line.

The series did not start on a home game system. It started in public places with game machines, in November 2012. An arcade is a public place with game machines, often in a shopping centre. Fishing Spirits was a medal game. In a medal game, players win medals, like small coins, and use them to keep playing.

So when people say "Fishing Spirits," they may mean the whole series. They may also mean one game. We will come back to this idea in Part 4.

Part 2: How the Arcade Game Works

In the arcade, you do not hold a usual game controller. You hold one shaped like a fishing rod. In front of you is a large screen with water and fish.

First, you throw your fishing line into the water. Then you wait for a fish to eat the food on the hook. You do not see the fish clearly at first. You only see a dark shape under the water. When a fish bites, the long stick in your hands shakes. Now you can see the whole fish. It might be small, or it might be very big.

Next, you turn the handle to bring the fish in. The handle pushes back against your hand, so it feels like a real fish is pulling. Some fish are hard to catch. For these fish, you can push a button for a lightning attack. This electric attack in the game helps you win the fight.

Tougher fish give you more points. The biggest fish are called Monster Class fish, and they are huge. More than one person can play on the machine, so friends can fish side by side.

Reading

Part 3: Made for Children, Enjoyed by Families

The company said it designed the game for children. This included children who had never been fishing in real life.

This was a problem for the designers. Real fishing can be difficult, and a small child may not know what to do. So the designers chose simple controls. A child only needs to cast, wait and turn the reel. At the same time, they made the screen and the rod look realistic. Because the controls are easy, a child can play quickly. Because the game looks real, it still feels exciting.

The company also described what happened around the machines. Children got very excited. Their parents often joined them. People who were walking past stopped to watch and cheer. These are the company's own observations, not a study of every player. Still, they show something important: one person plays, but many people can share the moment.

Part 4: From the Arcade to the Nintendo Switch

Later, the series moved from arcades into people's homes. This happened in two steps.

The first Switch game came out in 2019. Its name was Ace Angler: Nintendo Switch Version. Then Bandai Namco made a sequel, a new game that follows an earlier one. The sequel is called Ace Angler: Fishing Spirits. In July 2022, the company announced it. It came out on October 27, 2022 in Japan and Asia, and on October 28, 2022 in the United States.

Here, the names can be confusing. "Fishing Spirits" is the name of the whole series, which began in 2012. "Ace Angler: Fishing Spirits" is only one game, from 2022. So the series is ten years older than this game. The 2022 game is a part of the series, not the whole series.

Reading

Part 5: Inside the 2022 Switch Game

Ace Angler: Fishing Spirits tries to bring the arcade feeling into the home. The game takes place in Marine Medal Mania. This is an amusement park built around an aquarium. Inside the park, there are different attractions.

Ace Angler+ is the arcade fishing game. You do not hold a rod here. Instead, you shake and spin the Joy-Con controllers to cast and reel. There is also a story mode, and in the story you help save seas that are dirty with pollution. There are party minigames with sea animals, like Gator Panic and Goldfish Scooping. Shark Fever is a medal-pusher game. There is also online competition with other players.

You win medals in the game from these attractions. Then you use the medals to get fish and to make your aquarium better. You can collect more than 250 species and show them in your own aquarium. Some are real and famous, like great white sharks and giant squid. With a viewing camera, you can look closely at them and learn about them. A fish expert, Karibu Suzuki, checked the fish facts.

The number of players changes with the mode. One to four people can play on one Switch. Online, two to four people can play together. The game teaches facts about sea animals. However, it does not teach all real fishing skills.

Part 6: Why People May Enjoy It

We should not invent sales numbers or rankings. But we can look at the game's features and find likely reasons for its appeal.

The first reason is easy controls. From the start, the series was made for children with no fishing experience. You do not need special skills to begin.

The second reason is movement. You move your body when you play. In the arcade, you hold a rod and turn a reel. On the Switch, you shake and spin the controllers. This makes the game feel active.

The third reason is surprise. A fish first appears as a shadow, so you do not know what is coming. When it bites, you may find a small fish or a Monster Class giant. A big catch feels like a reward.

The fourth reason is people. In the arcade, parents joined their children and others cheered. On the Switch, friends and family can play party games or compete online.

The last reason is variety. Some players love catching fish. Others enjoy collecting animals for their aquarium and learning about them. Some want to finish the story. Not every player likes the same thing, but the game offers many different kinds of fun.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

Fishing Spirits started as a simple arcade game for children in 2012 and later moved to the Nintendo Switch. The 2022 game, Ace Angler: Fishing Spirits, is one part of this longer series. Easy controls, surprise, sharing and collecting all help explain why people may enjoy it.

  1. Would you rather play this kind of game in an arcade or at home? Why?

  2. What makes a game good for both children and adults?

  3. Is a fishing game a good way to learn about the sea? What can it teach, and what can it not teach?
