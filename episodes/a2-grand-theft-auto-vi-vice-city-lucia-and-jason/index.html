Part 1: The Series and Its Maker

Grand Theft Auto VI belongs to the Grand Theft Auto series. A series is a group of games with the same main name. This is the eighth main game in the series. It follows Grand Theft Auto V, which came out in 2013.

Rockstar Games makes and publishes the new game. This means it creates the game and brings it to players. Rockstar Games is an American video game company. It started in 1998 and is based in New York City. The company is also known for the Red Dead Redemption series.

Reading

Part 2: Leonida and Vice City

The game takes place in Leonida. Leonida is a made-up US state based on Florida. “Based on” means that a real place gives ideas for a made-up place. Leonida is not another name for the real state.

The game's world includes Vice City. This made-up city is based on Miami, a real city in Florida. Think of these places at two different sizes. Leonida is the state, and Vice City is a city inside it. The game has an open world. Players can explore its setting rather than only follow one fixed path.

Part 3: Lucia and Jason

The two main characters are Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval. Main characters are the people at the centre of a story. Their names often become important when people talk about a game.

Lucia is the Grand Theft Auto series' first main female character. The new game has two main characters, while Grand Theft Auto V has three: Michael, Franklin and Trevor. This gives us one clear difference between the games. Grand Theft Auto VI is also a single-player game. That means one person plays, even though the story has two main characters.

Part 4: A Different World and Things to Do

Grand Theft Auto V is set in Los Santos, a city based on Los Angeles. Grand Theft Auto VI changes the setting to Leonida and Vice City. Its map is reported to be three times larger than the area in Red Dead Redemption 2. This size comparison is with that game, not with Grand Theft Auto V.

Activities include skydiving, basketball and scuba diving. There is also a phone social media app called Snapmatic. The radio offers pop, country, hip-hop and Latin music, plus podcasts. These features give players different things to do and hear while exploring.

Part 5: The Trailers and Their Views

A trailer is a short video that gives people an early look at a game. Grand Theft Auto VI's first trailer came out in December 2023. The second came out in May 2025. Both appeared before the game's planned release.

The second trailer received a record 475 million views in 24 hours. That is almost half a billion views in one day. The number describes views, not the number of different people who watched. A view count shows how often a video was watched. It does not tell us what every viewer thought about it.

Part 6: Platforms and the Release Date

Grand Theft Auto VI is due to come out on 19 November 2026. Rockstar Games' official page gives this date. The game was delayed twice. A delay means that something happens later than first planned.

The announced platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A platform is the system that a game runs on. Here, these are game consoles. No PC version has been announced. That is different from saying a PC version will never exist. For now, the announced release is for those consoles, and the release date is still in the future.
