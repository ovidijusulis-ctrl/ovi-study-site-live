Part 1: A volleyball story

Haikyu!! is a Japanese manga about volleyball. A manga is a story told through words and pictures. Haruichi Furudate wrote and drew it. It appeared in the magazine Weekly Shonen Jump from 2012 to 2020. The complete manga has 45 volumes, or books.

Production I.G made the TV anime. The anime brings the players, games, and sounds to the screen. By December 2025, the manga had over 75 million copies in circulation. Its story follows players who learn to use their different skills together.

Part 2: Hinata finds a dream

Shoyo Hinata is short, but he is very fast and can jump very high. While he is in junior high school, he sees Karasuno High School play volleyball on TV. One short player catches his attention. This player is called the Little Giant.

Hinata feels inspired. He wants to play like him. However, Hinata’s own volleyball club has almost no members. His dream starts before he has a strong team. His height does not stop him from wanting to play. His speed and jump give him something important to build on.

Part 3: Kageyama becomes a partner

Part 4: Karasuno’s road back

Karasuno High School once had a strong volleyball team. The Little Giant played for Karasuno at the national tournament. Later, the team was no longer as strong. Other teams began calling it the Wingless Crows. The name suggests birds that cannot fly.

Now Karasuno wants to return to the Nationals, a tournament with teams from across Japan. Hinata and Kageyama become part of this effort. The goal is bigger than their personal rivalry. Karasuno needs its players to work together so the team can become strong again.

Part 5: How volleyball works

In indoor volleyball, each team has six players on the court. A net separates the teams. A rally starts with a serve from behind the end line. The teams then send the ball back and forth.

A team has up to three touches before returning the ball. A common pattern is receive, set, and spike. Players receive the ball, set it up, then hit it over the net. Every rally gives one team a point. This is rally scoring. A team scores when the ball lands on the other side or the other team makes a mistake. Most sets go to 25 points, with a two-point lead needed to win.

Sample answer

The usual pattern is receive, set, and spike. In rally scoring, one team gets a point after every rally.

Think one step further

Reading

Part 6: Different jobs, one team

Players have different jobs. The setter sets up the ball. A wing spiker attacks from the sides near the net. A middle blocker plays near the centre of the net and blocks or attacks. A libero helps defend in the back row and wears a different-colour shirt. A block is a jump at the net to stop an attack.

Hinata and Kageyama use a quick attack. Kageyama sends a low, fast set while Hinata is already jumping. Hinata’s speed and jump meet Kageyama’s skill. They need good timing and trust. This is why Haikyu!! is about teamwork. One player’s strength becomes more useful when it connects with another player’s strength.
