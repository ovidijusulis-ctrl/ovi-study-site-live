Four NBA Teams, Four Ways to Win

Introduction

First we look at the Boston Celtics and their long run of titles. Next come the Los Angeles Lakers, a team that moved cities, and then the Chicago Bulls and their two groups of three titles.

After that we meet the Golden State Warriors and their record seasons. At the end, we put all four teams side by side.

Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. Do you have a favourite sports team? Why do you like them?

  2. Is it better to win one big game or many small games? Why?

  3. If a team wins every year, is the sport still fun to watch?

Reading

Part 1: Boston Celtics – Many Titles Close Together

Imagine your school team wins the final this year. Then it wins next year, and the year after that. Now imagine it wins eight years in a row. That really happened to the Boston Celtics.

The Celtics started in 1946. They were one of the first eight teams in the NBA. Their name, the Celtics, comes from the Irish history of many families in Boston. Today they play at a building called TD Garden.

The Celtics have won 18 NBA championships. A championship is the title for the best team at the end of a season. But look at when the titles came. From 1959 to 1966, the Celtics won every year. Then they won again in 1968 and 1969. Bill Russell played for the team and also coached it at the same time.

So, many Celtics titles came close together, in about ten years. Their 18th title came much later, in 2024. Two years before that, in 2022, they played in the Finals but lost to the Warriors.

Check your understanding

Check: Part 1: Boston Celtics – Many Titles Close Together

1. Why is the Celtics' title number not the full story?

Sample answer

Because many of their 18 titles came close together, with eight in a row from 1959 to 1966, and the 18th came much later, in 2024.

Think one step further

Would you prefer a team that wins many years in a row, or a team that wins at different times? Why?

Reading

Part 2: Los Angeles Lakers – A Team That Moved

The Celtics stayed in one city. The Lakers did not. Their story is also a story about travel.

The team started in 1946 with a different name: the Detroit Gems. Later the team moved to Minneapolis. Then, in 1960, it moved again, to Los Angeles. So the Lakers have lived in three cities.

The Lakers have 17 NBA championships. Only the Celtics have more. Because these two teams are so close in titles, their games against each other always feel important.

One big moment came after 1979. That year, the Lakers chose a new young player, Magic Johnson. Later, the Lakers won a championship by beating the Celtics in Boston, in the Celtics' own city. For Lakers fans, that win meant more than a normal title.

So the Lakers' 17 titles are not only a number. They belong to a team that changed cities and beat its biggest rival in Boston.

1. Which three cities have been home to the Lakers?

Sample answer

Detroit first, then Minneapolis, and then Los Angeles from 1960.

Think one step further

Reading

Part 3: Chicago Bulls – Two Groups of Three

The Chicago Bulls have fewer titles than the Celtics or the Lakers. But they won them in a very short time.

The Bulls won six NBA championships between 1991 and 1998. First, they won three championships in a row, from 1991 to 1993. Then they stopped for a short time. After that, they won three championships in a row again, from 1996 to 1998. So the Bulls won three in a row two times.

One shot helped finish the first group of three. In 1993, the Bulls played the Phoenix Suns in the Finals. In Game 6, John Paxson made a three-point shot, and the Bulls won the game. That shot gave them their third title in a row.

So the Bulls show something new. A team does not need many titles to be special. Six titles in a short time, in two clear groups, makes a strong story too.

Check: Part 3: Chicago Bulls – Two Groups of Three

1. What did John Paxson's shot in 1993 do for the Bulls?

Sample answer

It won Game 6 of the Finals against the Phoenix Suns and gave the Bulls their third title in a row.

Think one step further

Reading

Part 4: Golden State Warriors – Records Inside a Season

The first three teams are often measured by titles. The Warriors also show another kind of strength: winning again and again inside one season.

The Warriors are third in NBA championships, after the Lakers and the Celtics. They are also third in Finals games played, after the same two teams.

Between 2015 and 2016, the Warriors started a season better than any NBA team before them. They also won game after game without losing, longer than any team before. These are NBA records. Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson played together near the back of the court. They helped make this time so strong.

The Warriors also met the Celtics in the 2022 Finals, and the Warriors won. So the third team on the list beat the team with the most titles.

1. What records did the Warriors set between 2015 and 2016?

Think one step further

Reading

Part 5: Side by Side – Count and Shape

Now let's put the four teams next to each other. First, count the titles. The Celtics have 18. The Lakers have 17. The Warriors are third. The Bulls have six.

But now look at the shape of the wins. The Celtics won eight in a row in the 1960s. The Lakers won while moving from city to city, and they beat the Celtics in Boston. The Bulls won six titles in two groups of three. The Warriors broke records inside one season and beat the Celtics in 2022.

So a number is like the score at the end of a game. It tells you who won, but not how they played. To understand a team, you need the number and the story together.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

At the start, you might think the title number tells you everything about a team. Now you know that the Celtics, Lakers, Bulls and Warriors each won in their own way. The number and the shape of the wins tell the full story together.

  1. If you started a new team, how would you like people to remember it?

  2. Think of another team you know. Does its title number tell the whole story?
