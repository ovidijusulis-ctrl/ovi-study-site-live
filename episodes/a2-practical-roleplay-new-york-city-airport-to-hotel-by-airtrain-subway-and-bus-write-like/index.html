Before you travel

Reading

First, choose your connection

At JFK, AirTrain is the first part of this journey into the city. It connects the airport terminals with Jamaica Station and Howard Beach.

Travel between airport terminals is free. Travel to Jamaica or Howard Beach has a separate fare.

For a journey towards Midtown Manhattan, Jamaica Station and the E train are a clear choice. Another option is Howard Beach and the A train.

These are different connections, so name the station when you ask for help. You can ask about Jamaica Station first, then ask which subway to take. This makes your question clear without a long explanation.

Check your understanding

Choose a clear question

1. You want to travel towards Midtown Manhattan. What connection could you ask about, and why should you name the station?

Sample answer

I could ask about AirTrain to Jamaica Station and then the E train. Naming Jamaica Station makes it clear which connection I want.

Think one step further

How would you ask a worker about that connection?

Useful language

Ask about your connection

Excuse me, I need help getting to Manhattan.
A polite way to ask for help with your route into the city.Excuse me, I need help getting to Manhattan. Can you show me the way?
Do I take AirTrain to Jamaica Station?
Check that AirTrain is the right first step.Do I take AirTrain to Jamaica Station, or another train?
Which subway do I take next?
Ask which subway line comes after AirTrain.Which subway do I take next after Jamaica Station?
So I take AirTrain first, then the E train. Thank you.
Repeat the route to confirm you understand.So I take AirTrain first, then the E train. Thank you for your help.

Reading

Pay for each part

AirTrain and the subway are separate parts of your journey, with separate fares. The AirTrain fare to Jamaica Station or Howard Beach is $8.75.

You pay that fare at those stations. You can use a contactless payment method or OMNY.

The subway fare is $3, and you tap at the turnstile. Together, these two fares are $11.75 for this airport and subway journey.

Keep the two payments clear when you ask for help. A useful question is whether a payment is for AirTrain or for the subway. You do not need a long speech to understand what you are paying for.

Check your understanding

Understand the payment

1. Someone explains the AirTrain payment. Why should you still ask about paying for the subway?

Sample answer

AirTrain and the subway have separate fares. Paying for AirTrain does not pay for the subway, so I still need to tap at the subway turnstile.

Useful language

Ask before you tap

Is this payment for AirTrain?
Check what this payment covers.Is this payment for AirTrain, or for the subway?
Can I pay with OMNY?
Ask if OMNY is accepted here.Can I pay with OMNY on this machine?
Where do I tap for the subway?
Ask where to tap your card to enter.Where do I tap for the subway at this station?
Do I use the same card on the bus?
Check if one card works for both subway and bus.Do I use the same card on the bus after the subway?

Reading

Keep the same card for a bus transfer

A local bus can be another part of a city journey after the subway. The subway and local bus fare is $3.

A free transfer is available within two hours when you use the same card or device. On the subway, you tap at the turnstile.

On a bus, you tap at the reader. If you paid with your phone on the subway, use that same device for the transfer.

Changing to a different card does not follow the same-card rule. Before choosing a bus, explain where you want to go. This example does not give a bus route to a particular hotel.

Make the transfer

1. You used your phone to pay for the subway and now want an eligible local bus transfer. What should you use on the bus, and why?

Sample answer

I should tap the same phone at the bus reader. The free transfer rule requires the same card or device within two hours.

Roleplay

Confirm your route

You

Excuse me. I want to get to Midtown Manhattan. Can you help?

Leo

Yes. Take AirTrain to Jamaica Station, then take the E train towards Manhattan.

You

Does AirTrain go from the airport terminals to Jamaica Station?

Leo

Yes, it connects the JFK terminals with Jamaica Station.

You

Is AirTrain free for that journey?

Leo

No. Travel between terminals is free, but the journey to Jamaica has a fare. You pay it at Jamaica Station.

You

All right. AirTrain to Jamaica Station, then the E train. Thank you.

Leo

That’s the connection for this journey.

Roleplay

Pay and plan your transfer

You

Hi. Is the payment here for AirTrain?

Rosa

Yes. The AirTrain fare at Jamaica Station is $8.75. The subway payment is separate.

You

Can I use OMNY for the subway?

Rosa

Yes. Tap at the subway turnstile. The subway fare is $3.

You

I may take a local bus after the subway. Can I use my phone for both?

Rosa

Yes. Use the same phone. A free transfer is available within two hours with the same card or device.

You

Where do I tap on the bus?

Rosa

At the bus reader.

You

Good. I’ll pay the AirTrain fare separately, then use the same phone for the subway and bus.

Rosa

Yes. That keeps your payments clear and follows the transfer rule.

Roleplay

Finish your journey

Maya

Welcome. How was your journey?

You

Good, thanks. I took AirTrain to Jamaica Station, then the E train towards Manhattan.

Maya

That is a clear connection towards Midtown. Did you also take a local bus?

You

Yes. I used the same phone within two hours for the free transfer.

Maya

Yes, that follows the transfer rule. What would you like now?

You

Could I have a coffee and a snack, please?

Maya

Of course. Here are your coffee and snack.

You

Thank you. I also have a reservation. I’d like to check in.

Maya

Please show me your booking confirmation.

You

Here it is.

Maya

Thank you. Your check-in is complete. Here is your room key.

You

Great. Thank you for your help.

Vocabulary

Useful Topic Vocabulary

connection
A way to change from one service to another.This is my subway connection.
fare
The money you pay for a journey.The subway has a separate fare.
turnstile
A gate you pass through to enter the subway.Tap at the turnstile.
transfer
A change from one form of transport to another.I want to make a bus transfer.
reservation
An arrangement to have a room or service ready for you.I have a reservation.
check in
Tell a hotel that you have arrived and get access to your room.I’d like to check in.
