Before you begin

Before You Arrive

  1. How do you usually travel from an airport?

  2. What help might you need with your bags?

  3. What do you enjoy talking about with someone new?

Reading

Choose Your Ride

You have your bags, and you are ready to leave Guam airport. The taxi counter is outside West Arrival.

Tumon hotels are a short taxi ride from the airport. Ask about the fare before you leave.

Taxis use meters, so do not guess the cost. A hotel transfer may be another choice.

Many Tumon hotels offer courtesy airport transfers, but you need to confirm this when you book. Do not assume your hotel has one.

Tell the driver your hotel name, and keep the address ready. A clear destination helps you begin the trip with less worry and enjoy your first conversation.

Check your understanding

Check: Choosing a Ride

1. You want a hotel transfer, but you have not confirmed one. What can you do, and what should you ask before taking a taxi?

Sample answer

A hotel transfer needs confirmation. Asking about the taxi fare helps you understand the cost before the ride.

Useful language

Useful Phrases: Your Ride

“I'd like a taxi to my hotel, please.”
Ask for a taxi ride from the airport to your hotel.“I'd like a taxi to my hotel, please. Here is the address.”
“Could you explain the fare before we leave?”
Politely ask how much the ride will cost before it starts.“Could you explain the fare before we leave? Do you use the meter?”
“Does my hotel offer an airport transfer?”
Ask if your hotel has a car or shuttle from the airport.“Does my hotel offer an airport transfer, or should I take a taxi?”
“I'm here on vacation.”
Tell someone you are traveling for fun, not for work.“I'm here on vacation for five days.”
“What do you do?”
Ask someone about their job.“What do you do? Do you work here in Guam?”
“Nice to meet you.”
A friendly thing to say when you meet someone for the first time.“Nice to meet you. Have a good trip.”

Reading

Ask for Help and Directions

After a flight, it is normal to need a little help. At Guam airport, car rental and tour counters are near baggage claim.

Hotel phones are near the customs exit. These are different places, so name the service you need.

If you want to contact your hotel, ask for the hotel phones. If you want a taxi, ask for the counter outside West Arrival.

You can start with a simple question and explain your reason. For example, you may need to ask your hotel about a transfer.

Thank the person who helps you. A short, friendly exchange can make arrival feel easier.

Check your understanding

Check: Getting Help

1. You need to call your hotel about a transfer. What would you ask, and where are the hotel phones?

Sample answer

Naming the service and giving your reason makes the request clear.

Useful language

Useful Phrases: Help and Conversation

“Excuse me. Where are the hotel phones?”
Ask where the airport phones for calling hotels are.“Excuse me. Where are the hotel phones? I need to call my hotel.”
“I need to call my hotel about a transfer.”
Say you want to ask your hotel about your ride from the airport.“I need to call my hotel about a transfer. Is there a phone here?”
“Thank you. That helps.”
Thank someone and say their help was useful.“Thank you. That helps a lot.”
“Where are you from?”
Ask which country or city someone comes from.“Where are you from? Are you from Japan too?”
“I'm from Canada.”
Say which country you come from. Use your own country.“I'm from Canada. How about you?”
“Are you here for work or a holiday?”
Ask if someone is traveling for their job or for fun.“Are you here for work or a holiday this time?”

Reading

A Friendly Travel Conversation

A travel conversation can begin with the trip you share. At Guam airport, you might meet another traveler near baggage claim.

You might also talk while waiting near the taxi counter outside West Arrival. Start with an easy question, such as where the person is from.

Then share a little about yourself. You can talk about your work, your holiday, or your plans.

A short answer is enough; you do not need a long story. Ask another question when the person seems happy to talk.

If they are tired, keep the conversation short. Friendly small talk gives both people space to speak.

Check: Keeping Conversation Friendly

1. Another traveler answers your question but looks tired. How can you keep the conversation friendly?

Sample answer

Friendly conversation includes noticing whether the other person wants to keep talking.

Reading

Arrive and Check In

Your airport trip ends when you reach your hotel, but another conversation begins. If you chose a taxi, remember that Guam taxis use meters.

If you planned a courtesy transfer, remember that many Tumon hotels offer one, but confirmation matters. At the hotel, explain that you want to check in.

Give the name on your reservation clearly. If you do not understand a question, ask the person to repeat it.

You can also share why you are visiting. Perhaps you want a quiet holiday or time to explore.

Keep personal answers simple. A friendly conversation can happen while you finish your arrival.

Check your understanding

Check: Clear Hotel Communication

1. You arrive at your hotel and do not understand the receptionist's question. What can you say before continuing?

Sample answer

Asking for repetition helps you answer the right question instead of guessing.

Useful language

Useful Phrases: At the Hotel

“I'd like to check in, please.”
Ask to start your hotel stay at the front desk.“I'd like to check in, please. Here is my passport.”
“The reservation is under my name.”
Tell the hotel that your booking uses your name.“The reservation is under my name. It's for four nights.”
“Could you say that again, please?”
Politely ask someone to repeat what they said.“Could you say that again, please? I didn't hear the room number.”
“I work in a shop.”
Tell someone about your job. Change “a shop” to your own job.“I work in a shop. I sell clothes.”
“I'd like to do some sightseeing.”
Say you want to visit interesting places.“I'd like to do some sightseeing tomorrow.”
“I'm here for a quiet holiday.”
Say you came to rest and relax.“I'm here for a quiet holiday at the beach.”

Speak

Make the Conversation Your Own

Arrival conversation: You are explaining your travel plan to a friend before flying to Guam. Give a short spoken answer: choose a taxi or a hotel transfer, explain what you need to ask or confirm, and say how you will get help at the airport. Then share where you are from, what you do, and why you want to visit. Use your own details, or clearly imagined ones. Finish with one friendly question for your friend.

fare

meter
a device that measures the taxi fareTaxis use meters.
