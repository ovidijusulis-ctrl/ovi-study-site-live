Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you like hot drinks or cold drinks?

    Need a starting line?

    Both hot and cold drinks are popular in cafes.

  2. What drink do you usually order at a cafe?

    Need a starting line?

    A latte is a popular choice at many cafes.

  3. Can you name one drink that does not have coffee in it?

    Need a starting line?

    Many cafes have lots of options without coffee.

Reading

Popular Cafe Drinks

Cafes offer a wide range of drinks. Espresso is a small, strong coffee. A latte mixes espresso with steamed milk and has a mild, creamy taste. A cappuccino is similar but has a thick layer of foam on top.

Not everyone wants coffee. Hot chocolate is sweet and popular in winter. Green tea is a light option with a gentle flavour. Many cafes also sell iced drinks. An iced latte is a latte served over ice. Fruit teas, like peach or mango tea, are sweet and cold. Most cafes let you choose the size: small, medium, or large.

Check: Cafe Drinks

1. What is the difference between a latte and a cappuccino?

Sample answer

Both use espresso and milk, but the amount of foam is different.

Think one step further

Which one do you think you would prefer?

Useful language

Useful Phrases: Talking About Drinks

I would like a latte, please.
A polite way to order a drink.I would like a latte, please. Medium size.
What size would you like?
The barista asks about the size of your drink.What size would you like? Small, medium, or large?
Can I have it iced?
You ask for the drink to be served cold over ice.Can I have it iced? It is hot today.
Is there milk in this?
You ask whether the drink contains milk.Is there milk in this? I prefer no milk.

Reading

How to Order at a Cafe

Ordering at a cafe usually follows a simple pattern. First, the barista greets you. You say what you want. The barista may ask about size, milk type, or extras.

For example, at Starbucks you choose a size first: tall (small), grande (medium), or venti (large). At a local cafe, you can just say small, medium, or large. After you order, the barista tells you the price. You pay, then wait for your name to be called or for the barista to bring your drink to the counter.

Most cafes are happy to make small changes to your drink. For example, you can swap regular milk for oat milk, or ask for no sugar. Keep any request short and clear.

Useful language

Useful Phrases: Ordering

Can I have oat milk instead?
You ask to change the type of milk in your drink.Can I have oat milk instead? I do not drink cow's milk.
No sugar, please.
You ask the barista not to add sugar.A green tea, please. No sugar, please.
For here or to go?
The barista asks if you will drink inside or take the drink away.For here or to go? I will drink it here.
How much is that?
You ask the total price.How much is that? I have cash.

Roleplay

Roleplay: Hot Drink Order

Barista

Good morning! What can I get for you today?

You

Good morning. I would like a latte, please.

Barista

Of course. What size would you like? Small, medium, or large?

You

What is the difference in price?

Barista

Small is the cheapest, and large is the biggest. Most people get medium.

You

Medium, please. And can I have oat milk instead of regular milk?

Barista

No problem at all. For here or to go?

You

For here, please. How much is that?

Barista

That is four fifty. I will call your name when it is ready.

Your goal is to ask about the fruit teas, choose one, and order it without sugar.
