Your Cafe Choices

  1. What do you usually drink at a cafe?

  2. Do you like cake or a cookie with your drink?

  3. Do you usually pay by card or with cash?

Reading

Drinks and Something Sweet

A cafe order can start with a drink and something sweet. At the cafe, Maya serves hot coffee and cold tea.

Coffee can come with milk, while the cold tea can come without sugar. These details matter because two people can order tea but want different drinks.

A small cup holds less than a large cup. Maya also serves cake by the slice and cookies as separate pieces.

A slice of cake and a cookie are two different dessert choices. One person might want only a drink, while another wants both.

The drink, its size, and the dessert are separate parts of the order. Clear details help Maya prepare the right things.

Check your understanding

Make the Order Clear

1. A customer wants cold tea and something sweet. What details would help Maya prepare the order?

Sample answer

The drink alone does not tell Maya its size, the sugar choice, or the dessert.

Useful language

Choose Your Drink and Dessert

I'd like a small hot coffee, please. A cold tea without sugar, please. I'd like a slice of cake, too. Just a cookie, please.

Reading

Build a Clear Order

An order is easier to follow when its details stay together. The drink name tells the cafe worker what to prepare.

Words such as small, hot, and cold make that choice clearer. At the cafe, Leo takes an order for a small hot coffee with milk.

He repeats the drink details before preparing it. The customer listens and confirms that the order is right.

A dessert can follow as another part of the same order. Leo then adds a cookie for this customer.

An order can also include whether the customer will stay or take it away. This customer will stay, so Leo now has the complete order.

Check your understanding

Listen to the Details

1. Leo repeats “a large hot coffee with milk,” but the customer wants a small one. How can the customer correct the order?

Sample answer

The customer changes the size and keeps the other drink details clear.

Useful language

Confirm or Change a Detail

A small one, please. With milk, please. Without sugar, please. That's right, thank you. To take away, please. I'll stay here.

Reading

Pay for the Right Order

Payment comes after the order is clear. Here, Maya accepts both card and cash.

A customer chooses how to pay before Maya finishes the sale. With cash, the customer gives money directly to Maya.

With a card, the customer uses the card machine. The bill should match the food and drinks the customer ordered.

In this example, Maya notices an extra cookie on the bill. She removes it before taking payment.

The customer then pays by card and gets a receipt. Fixing the order first keeps the payment clear for both people.

Before You Pay

1. Your bill includes a cookie you did not order. What should happen before you pay?

Sample answer

The bill needs to match the order before payment.

Useful language

Pay and Fix a Small Mistake

Can I pay by card? I'll pay with cash. Sorry, I didn't order this cookie. Just the coffee, please. Could I have a receipt, please?

Roleplay

Choose the Size

Maya

Hello! What would you like?

You

I'd like a hot coffee with milk, please.

Maya

Small or large?

You

Small, please.

Maya

Will you stay here or take it away?

You

To take away, please.

Maya

That's a small hot coffee with milk to take away.

You

That's right, thank you.

Maya

Here is your coffee.

Scene

Cold Tea and Cake

You are choosing a drink and dessert with Leo at the cafe, which serves cold tea and slices of cake. You want a small cold tea without sugar and a slice of cake to enjoy there.

Your goal is to order both items and make the sugar choice clear.

Something Sweet on the Side

Leo

Hello! What would you like?

You

A small cold tea and a slice of cake, please.

Leo

Would you like sugar in your tea?

You

No sugar, please.

Leo

Will you stay here?

You

Yes, I'll stay here.

Leo

So, a small cold tea without sugar and a slice of cake.

You

Yes, that's right.

Leo

Here are your tea and cake. Enjoy!

Roleplay

Correct the Bill and Pay

Maya

Your bill is ready.

You

Sorry, I didn't order this cookie. Just the coffee, please.

Maya

I'm sorry. I've removed the cookie. The bill now has only your coffee.

You

Thank you. Can I pay by card?

Maya

Yes. We accept card and cash. Please use the card machine.

You

I've paid. Could I have a receipt, please?

Maya

Your payment is complete. Here is your receipt.

You

Thank you. Goodbye!

Speak

Make Your Own Order

  1. You are placing an order at a cafe. Choose a coffee or tea and a cake slice or cookie. Say your complete order aloud, including size, hot or cold, and milk or sugar. Then say whether you will stay or take it away and how you want to pay.

    Need a starting line?
    • The worker repeats the wrong size. Say a short correction aloud.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

  1. A clear cafe order includes the drink and the details you want. The bill should match your order before you pay. Which details matter most for your usual cafe order, and what would you say if the bill were wrong?

Vocabulary

Useful Topic Vocabulary

drink
Something you have from a cup or glass.I'd like a cold drink.
dessert
Sweet food, such as cake.I'd like a dessert with my tea.
slice
A flat piece cut from something larger.A slice of cake, please.
cookie
A small, sweet baked food.I'd like a cookie, too.
cash
Money in coins or notes.I'll pay with cash.
card
A bank card used to pay for things.Can I pay by card?
bill
A list showing what you need to pay for.The bill includes a cookie.
receipt
A record showing that you paid.Could I have a receipt, please?
