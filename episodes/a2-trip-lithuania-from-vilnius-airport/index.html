Introduction

Your plane lands in Vilnius, the capital of Lithuania. You are tired, but you are happy too.

You have five questions: how to pay, how to get to the city, what to see, how to check in and what to eat. In this simulation, you take each step in time order.

You learn real prices and real times. You also practise simple English for each step.

Before you begin

Warm-up

  1. When you land in a new country, what do you do first?

  2. Do you like the train, the bus or a taxi from an airport? Why?

  3. What food do you want to try in a new country?

Reading

Part 1: You Land at Vilnius Airport

You land at Vilnius International Airport. This airport is the main airport for Vilnius. Many airlines fly here. You walk with the other people to passport control. You wait in a line. An officer looks at your passport. Then you take your bag.

Next, you think about money. Prices in Vilnius are in euros (€). For example, a bus ticket from the airport to the city centre costs €1. You buy this bus ticket from the driver. So it is good to have your payment ready before you get on the bus.

Your phone is important too. You need maps and the Bolt app. So you look for a SIM card. You do not know the price yet. Ask at the shop before you buy.

Check your understanding

Check: Part 1: You Land at Vilnius Airport

1. Why is it a good idea to have your payment ready before you leave the airport?

Sample answer

Prices in Vilnius are in euros. You buy the €1 bus ticket from the driver, so you must pay right away when you get on the bus.

Reading

Part 2: You Choose a Ride to the City

You have three good ways to get to the city. Each way has a different time and price.

The first way is the train. The train goes from the airport to the central railway station. The trip is 4.3 km long. It takes about 7 or 8 minutes. A ticket costs €0.45, and you can buy it online. This train is fast and cheap. But it runs only 16 times a day. So check the next train time first.

The second way is the bus. The bus takes about 15 minutes to the city centre. Several buses go to the city centre and the Old Town. You can buy a €1 ticket from the driver. City tickets cost €1 for 30 minutes or €1.25 for 60 minutes.

The third way is a taxi or a ride app. A taxi from the airport to Cathedral Square in the Old Town costs about €10. It takes about 15 minutes. You find taxis at the arrivals area. Bolt is a big ride company in Vilnius. Bolt and Uber work 24 hours a day, every day. Open the app and check the price before you say yes.

Check: Part 2: You Choose a Ride to the City

1. You land very late at night. The next train is not soon. What can you do, and why?

Sample answer

I can take a taxi or use Bolt or Uber. They work 24 hours a day. A taxi to Cathedral Square costs about €10 and takes about 15 minutes.

Reading

Part 3: Your First Look at the Old Town

You arrive in the city. You walk into Vilnius Old Town. The Old Town is very old. Many of its streets and buildings are hundreds of years old. It is one of the largest old towns in Northern Europe. So you see many small streets and many old buildings.

You see two big squares. One is Cathedral Square. The other is Town Hall Square. You also see the buildings of Vilnius University. They are a famous part of the Old Town.

Then you see the Gate of Dawn. This is an important old gate. People built it between 1503 and 1514. It was part of the city wall. Later, people took down most of the city walls. But they kept this gate. It has a famous picture of the Virgin Mary. Many people come here to pray. The church services are in Lithuanian and Polish.

Reading

Part 4: You Check In and Ask for Help

You find your hotel. You go to the front desk. You say your name. You show your passport. The staff member gives you a key card. Now you have a room.

You are new in Vilnius, so ask for help. The front desk is a good place for questions. Ask for the Wi-Fi password. Ask for a map. Ask for the way to Cathedral Square or Town Hall Square. These are big squares in the Old Town, so they are easy places to meet people or start a walk.

You can ask about food too. Hotel staff know the area.

Reading

Part 5: Your First Lithuanian Meal

You go to a restaurant near your hotel. You look at the menu.

You see two famous Lithuanian dishes. The first dish is cepelinai. They are big potato dumplings, often with meat inside. The cook boils them. They come with a sour cream sauce and small bits of bacon or pork rind. This is a warm, heavy meal. It is good after a long trip.

The second dish is šaltibarščiai. This is a cold soup, and it is bright pink. It has boiled and cooled beetroot, cucumber, green onion, dill and other herbs. The beetroot gives it the pink colour. People in Vilnius love this soup. Since 2023, Vilnius has a festival for this soup every year.

The menu shows the prices in euros. Read the price before you order.

Conversation: At the Airport Information Desk

Practise with the dialogue audio

You

Hello. How can I get to the Old Town?

Rūta

Hello! You have three choices. You can take the train, the bus or a taxi.

You

How long does it take?

Rūta

The train takes about 8 minutes to the railway station. The bus and the taxi take about 15 minutes.

You

The train is fast! How much is a ticket?

Rūta

The train ticket costs €0.45. You can buy it online. But the train runs only 16 times a day. Please check the next train time.

You

Oh, I see. And the bus?

Rūta

The bus ticket costs €1. Several buses go to the city centre and the Old Town.

You

Where can I buy a ticket?

Rūta

You can buy it from the driver.

You

And a taxi? I am a little tired.

Rūta

A taxi to Cathedral Square costs about €10. Taxis wait at arrivals. You can also use Bolt or Uber. They work all day and all night.

You

Thank you. I think I want the bus. It is cheap, and it goes to the Old Town.

Rūta

Good choice. Is this your first time in Lithuania?

You

Yes, it is. I am from Japan.

Rūta

Welcome to Vilnius! Enjoy the Old Town.

You

Thank you very much. Goodbye!

Speak

Speaking

Work with a partner. Tell the story of your first day in Vilnius. Use "I" and the present tense. Start at the airport and end at dinner. Choose the train, the bus or a taxi, and say the time and the price. Say one thing about the Old Town or the Gate of Dawn. Then order one dish. Your partner asks two questions, for example "Why do you choose the bus?" and "What is in the soup?" Then change roles.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

You go from Vilnius Airport to your hotel in about 15 minutes, and the trip can cost €0.45, €1 or about €10. You see a very old town, check in with simple questions and try two famous Lithuanian dishes. Now you can plan this first day and talk about it in English.

  1. Which ride from the airport do you choose, and why?

  2. What do you want to see first in the Old Town?

  3. Do you want to try cepelinai or šaltibarščiai first? Why?
