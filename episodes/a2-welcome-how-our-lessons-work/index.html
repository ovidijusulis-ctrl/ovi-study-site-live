Reading

Part 2: Listen and Read

At the top of the page, there are three buttons: Study, Listen and Read. Study is the full lesson with check questions.

In Read, you see the text in small pieces. In Listen, a voice reads the lesson to you.

The words light up when you hear them. So you can listen and read at the same time.

Each part also has a Play this part button. Tap it to hear only that part.

You can change the help language, too. Use the English, 日本語 or Español button.

Part 3: Questions and Practice

After each part, there is a short question. Answer it first, then read the answer below. Some lessons also have practice games. There are six kinds of exercise:

  • Choose: pick the right answer.
  • Match: join the two halves.
  • Build: make a sentence from words.
  • Fix: find the wrong word.
  • Type: write the answer.
  • Transform: change a sentence.

You always try first, and the answer comes after. You can stop and come back later. Your results are saved. Try the practice below now!

    Part 5: Lessons Made for You

    At Ovi English, each lesson is written for one student. It starts from what you like and what you need.
