Introduction

Many games are fun and free to start. But the people who make them need money.

Roblox, Fortnite, and phone games earn money in different ways. A smart player also thinks before buying.

Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you play Roblox, Fortnite, or another game?

  2. Have you seen a shop inside a game?

  3. What can people buy in a game?

Reading

Part 1 — Why Games Need Money

Games are fun, but they are also a business. A team works every day to make every game, and some people write code or draw pictures.

Other people make music, fix problems, and help players. The company must pay these workers and make new things.

A server lets people play online, and the company pays for it. Some games cost money when you buy them.

Roblox and Fortnite are free to start. They need other ways to make money over time.

Check — Part 1

1. Why do game companies need money?

Sample answer

They need money to pay workers, make new things, and run the game online.

Reading

Part 2 — Shops Inside Games

Free games often have a shop inside the game. Players use real money to buy game money.

Roblox calls its game money Robux, and Fortnite calls its game money V-Bucks. Players can buy skins, characters, coins, and other items.

A skin only changes how a character looks. Fortnite also sells a Battle Pass with rewards.

Some passes give players more things as they play. These things can be fun, but players do not need them to play.

Check — Part 2

1. How can a free game make money?

Sample answer

It can sell game money, skins, items, or a pass. It can also use ads.

Reading

Part 3 — Ads and Special Events

Games can make money from ads too. In many phone games, a player can watch a short ad.

The player may get coins or an extra life. The game company gets money when people watch.

Roblox can also show ads inside a game world. Games also have events with rare or limited items.

These items feel special because they are not always there. Many players come back and may buy something during the event.

Check — Part 3

1. How do ads and events help a game make money?

Sample answer

The company gets money when people watch ads. Events bring players back, and some players buy something.

Reading

Part 4 — Updates and Smart Spending

Game companies want players to stay interested for a long time. They add new maps, characters, missions, and rewards.

Fortnite often starts a new season with new things. An update can make the game feel fresh and bring many players back.

A shop may say that an item is here for a short time. This can make a player want to buy it now.

It is smart to stop, check the real price, and think before you buy. Buy only something you will use and can pay for.
