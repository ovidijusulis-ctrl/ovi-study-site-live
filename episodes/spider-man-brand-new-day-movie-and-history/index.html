Before you begin

Warm-Up Questions

  1. Do you know any Spider-Man stories? What do you remember?

    Need a starting line?

    There are no wrong answers here. Just think about what you already know.

  2. If people forgot who you were, how would you feel?

    Need a starting line?

    This helps you connect to Peter Parker's situation in the new movie.

  3. What makes someone a hero? Think of one reason.

    Need a starting line?

    Keep this idea in mind as you learn about Spider-Man's choices.

Reading

Part 1 — How Spider-Man Began

Spider-Man first appeared in Amazing Fantasy 15 in August 1962. Stan Lee wrote the stories and Steve Ditko drew them.

In the story, a student named Peter Parker visits a science exhibit. A radioactive spider bites him.

After the bite, Peter gains super powers. He invents web shooters. He puts on a costume and becomes Spider-Man.

Part 2 — The World Forgets Peter

Peter is alone. But he does not stop.

What you can do now

Final Reflection

  1. Peter Parker loses everything people know about him. But he still chooses to be Spider-Man. Do you think that is brave? What would you do?

    Need a starting line?

    There is no single right answer. The important thing is to think about what courage means to you.

    • Which part of Peter's story — his origin, his loneliness, or his hidden enemy — surprised you most? Why?

Vocabulary

Useful Topic Vocabulary

radioactive
giving off energy from atoms; can change living thingsA radioactive spider bit Peter Parker and gave him super powers.
origin
the start or beginning of somethingAmazing Fantasy 15 tells the origin of Spider-Man.
villain
a bad person who causes harm or dangerThe villain in Brand New Day cannot be seen.
full-time
doing something as your main job, with all your timePeter works as Spider-Man full-time after the world forgets him.
transformation
a change from one thing into something differentA transformation begins in Peter that he may not be able to control.
threat
something that may cause harm or dangerPeter faces a new threat in Brand New Day.
pressure
a feeling that pushes or forces you to changeSeeing his old friends move on creates pressure for Peter.
courage
the ability to do something hard or scaryIt takes courage to keep helping people when no one knows your name.
