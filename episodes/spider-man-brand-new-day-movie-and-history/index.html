The New Movie

Spider-Man: Brand New Day arrives in cinemas on July 31, 2026.

Tom Holland plays Peter Parker, and Zendaya is also in the film.

Sadie Sink and Jacob Batalon are part of the cast too.

Destin Daniel Cretton directs the movie.

In this story, Peter fights crime every day.

Nobody in the city remembers who he is.

He sees old friends living their lives, and this makes things harder for him.

A dangerous villain threatens the city, but no one can see this villain.

Peter faces big changes he may not be able to control.

Sony Pictures lists the film and credits the original Marvel comic by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko.

Reading

How Spider-Man Began

This short history of Spider Man starts in 1962.

Writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko created him together.

His first story appeared in a comic called Amazing Fantasy number 15.

Peter Parker is a teenager.

He lives with his Aunt May and his Uncle Ben.

One day, Peter visits a science exhibit.

A radioactive spider bites him there, and he gets special powers.

He can climb walls, and he has super strength.

It was the first Spider-Man story.

The Library of Congress in the United States now keeps original art from Amazing Fantasy number 15.
