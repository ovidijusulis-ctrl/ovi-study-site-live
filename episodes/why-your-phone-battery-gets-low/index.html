Reading

What Is Inside Your Phone Battery

Many modern phones use a rechargeable lithium-ion battery.

It stores energy for the phone.

Each time you charge and use the battery, it changes a little.

After many months or years, it cannot hold as much energy as before.

Apple calls this chemical ageing.

It is a normal part of battery life.

Reading

Heat Is Bad for Your Battery

Heat is hard on a phone battery.

A hot battery can lose power quickly, and heat can make it age faster.

Do not leave your phone in a hot car or in strong sun.

If the phone gets very hot, move it to a cooler place and let it cool.

Reading

Screen Brightness and Apps Use Power

The screen and busy apps can use a lot of energy.

A bright screen needs more power.

Video, maps, and games can also use power quickly.

Lower the screen brightness and let the screen turn off sooner.

Your battery settings can show which apps use the most power.

Reading

Good Charging Habits

You can charge your phone when you need to.

You do not have to wait until the battery is at zero, and you do not have to charge it to 100 percent every time.

Use the charger made for your phone or another charger that the maker says is safe to use.

When you need more time, turn on Battery Saver or Low Power Mode.
